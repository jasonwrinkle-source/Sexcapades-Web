The app now keeps itself current: every load checks for the newest version, and installed copies update themselves. You should never be stuck on an old version — and you never need to clear your browser’s cache or delete browsing data for this site.

One exception: if you installed the app or visited before October 20, 2026, your device may still hold one older copy. A hard refresh — a reload that forces the browser to fetch the fresh copy instead of reusing the stored one — clears it, once. After that, the app stays current on its own:

  • Windows or Linux: press Ctrl + Shift + R.
  • Mac: press Cmd + Shift + R.
  • Chromebook: press Ctrl + Shift + R.
  • Phone or tablet: close the app (or the browser tab) completely, then reopen it.
  • If the page still won’t load at all, check your connection and try again in a few minutes.

Saving a FetLife profile page? Save it as a single file. On your profile, expand the Fetishes section’s subsections, then save the page (Ctrl+S) and choose Webpage, Single File (.mhtml). Don’t choose “HTML Only” — that format leaves the Fetishes section out of the file entirely, and the importer will report that no Fetishes heading was found.

Sexcapades uses four separate color systems. Their meanings overlap in places, but they are not interchangeable:

  1. Rating slider colors describe how you rated a topic. These same ratings appear on the map’s At-a-Glance rings and in list view:
    GreenWant to do / interested
    YellowCurious / maybe
    RedNot interested / limit
  2. Net colors show the active interest-threshold filter, not a new rating. Red is selective and keeps only your strongest interests; yellow is the middle; green is open and casts the widest net, showing more topics. Changing Net only filters what is visible.
  3. Hanky code colors identify activities, not your level of interest. The hanky code is a traditional handkerchief color-coding system that originated in gay and biker culture for signaling sexual interests. A person might wear a colored handkerchief in a back pocket at a bar, club, munch, or play party so others can recognize those interests. Sexcapades uses those traditional colors on topic branches where a matching code exists. Open the hanky code chart.
  4. Cast colors describe comparison strictness, not anyone’s rating: red is a narrow, strict cast with fewer, stronger matches; yellow is the middle; green is a wide, lenient cast with more potential matches. The cyan tab outline and Cast indicator show which profiles or control are selected—they are not compatibility scores.

Still stuck?

These three checks solve most problems.

  1. Hard-refresh the page (Ctrl + Shift + R) so you’re viewing the current version.
  2. Confirm your import came directly from a Sexcapades export or sync card.
  3. Keep an original Sexcapades PNG, SVG, PDF, or JSON export as your permanent backup.
