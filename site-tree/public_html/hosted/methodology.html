Statistical Methodology

How Sexcapades computes and displays community ratings statistics.

Rating Histograms

Each topic's community ratings are displayed as a histogram with 12 bins across the −100 to +100 rating scale. We chose histograms over simple averages because kink ratings are rarely normally distributed — a topic averaging 0 could mean "everyone's lukewarm" or "half love it, half hate it." The histogram shows you which.

Bin Width

Bin width selection involves both mathematical rules of thumb (Sturges' rule, Freedman-Diaconis, Scott's rule) and aesthetic judgment. With enough data, the underlying shape emerges regardless of the exact bin choice. Our 12-bin default will be revisited as the dataset grows.

Privacy Threshold

Histograms are only displayed once a topic has at least 20 community ratings. Below this threshold, the dialog shows how many ratings have been submitted so far. This prevents any individual's ratings from being identifiable.

Marker Percentages

Markers (Favorite ★, Hard Limit ×, Not Interested ⊘, Fantasy Only, Already Tried) are categorical, not continuous, so they're reported as percentages with Wilson score 95% confidence intervals.

We use the Wilson interval rather than the normal approximation because it's more accurate for small sample sizes and extreme percentages, and it never produces impossible values (below 0% or above 100%).

Example: "12% ±3% favorite" means the true population percentage is likely between 9% and 15%.

Community Completion

We also track how far contributors get through the map — not who they are, just how many topics get rated. This tells us whether people do a quick pass or go deep, and whether returning contributors rate more over time.

Rating Pace

We also measure how fast contributors rate — topics per minute and average elapsed time by how many topics were rated. This describes real sessions, not idealized ladder rungs: some people rate 4 per category, others do the full map in one sitting.

What We Don't Do

  • We never display individual ratings or identify who rated what.
  • We never combine your personal ratings with community data without your knowledge.
  • All community statistics are computed from anonymized, aggregated survey submissions.
Note: These statistics are descriptive, not prescriptive. A kink being statistically uncommon doesn't make it wrong — it makes it yours.
