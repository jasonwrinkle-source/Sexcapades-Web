Triad Topics & 2D Barycentric Rating

While most topics on the hypermap are two-pole pairs or single interest sliders, certain positions, dynamics, and structures exist as three-way systems. Sexcapades models these as Triad Topics.

The Three Types of Triad Topics

Triad topics in the taxonomy fall into three distinct structural types, each with its own conceptual meaning:

  1. Relational Identity & Dynamic Balance Triads (e.g., Bull / Hotwife / Cuckold, Vixen / Stag / Cuckquean, Top / Side / Bottom). The relationship-geometry content that once sat in a three-way form now lives in Intimacy geometry, a four-pole diamond.

    These topics define core relationship roles or sexual orientations where an individual or dynamic sits within a 3-pole spectrum. Rather than three independent yes/no toggles, the roles form a coupled identity space where your orientation is defined by your balance among the three vertices. For these identity nodes, Sexcapades provides the 2D Barycentric Triangle Widget. The triangle vertices represent the relative weights of the three poles (such that \(w_0 + w_1 + w_2 = 1.0\)), while an adjacent intensity slider (or mouse wheel) adjusts overall desire or salience (\(-100\) to \(+100\)).

  4. Independent Multi-Role Triples (practices with three discrete unconstrained roles or skills).

    Here, the three poles represent distinct capabilities or activities that can be pursued independently without trade-offs. You might be interested in one, two, or all three equally without shifting a shared balance point.

How Barycentric Coordinates & Similarity Scoring Work

When rating with the 2D triangle widget, your position inside the triangle determines your barycentric coordinate \((w_0, w_1, w_2)\) across the three colored poles (Red, Green, and Blue). Dragging the puck toward a corner increases that pole's relative weight, while positioning it in the center represents an equal balance (\(33.3\%\) each). The intensity slider sets the magnitude of your desire (positive for interest, negative for aversion or limits).

  • 2D Barycentric Distance: Measures how closely two profiles share the same internal role balance. In Euclidean space, the distance \(d = \sqrt{(x_a - x_b)^2 + (y_a - y_b)^2}\) quantifies identity alignment.
  • Complementary Role Matching: In dynamics that rely on interlocking roles (such as a Bull partnering with a Hotwife and a Cuckold, or a Top connecting with a Bottom), compatibility is scored by pairing complementary poles.
