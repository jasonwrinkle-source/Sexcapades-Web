Hypermap

Your visual profile — a radial map of a topic set, color-coded by your ratings. On Sexcapades the map holds 400+ kink topics; on the vanilla skins it holds that skin's topics — politics, faith, physics, and the rest. Not a "kink map."

Quick Start

Rate 3 topics per category (54 total) in ~20 minutes. Gives you a partial hypermap you can already compare or turn into a Scribe story. The full 400+ topics are optional.

Net

The old name for Veil.

See Veil.

Cast

Retired.

The old compare control: a button with a strictness slider that built a combined view from multi-selected profiles. The app now mints a group profile directly when you multi-select tabs. See Group profile.

Scribe

Story generator. Turn your hypermap into an erotic story (single profile) or a play guide (multiple profiles). Works with a partial Quick Start map — no partner required.

Scribe vs Scene Setter: Scribe writes scenes and stories (creative). Scene Setter plans and negotiates play (practical). Use Scribe for fantasy, Scene Setter for real-world coordination.

Scene Setter

Play planner. Plan and negotiate a scene with a partner — boundaries, safewords, aftercare. Practical coordination, not storytelling. Pair with Scribe: Scene Setter plans it, Scribe writes it.

Scan or Import

Previously called "Scan a Sync Card."

The dialog for importing profiles. Three ways in: (1) scan a QR code with your camera, (2) upload a JPEG/PNG image of a QR code, (3) upload an exported file (PNG, SVG, PDF, sync card). JSON files are also accepted.

Sync Card

Business-card-size (3.5"×2") physical card with your profile QR codes. Front has a QR linking to the scanner; back has your profile data as numbered QR codes. Print and hand out at munches.

QR Sheet

Not "QR page."

A page of QR codes (black on white, one per image) for sharing in online galleries or picture sets. Each sheet has the deep link printed on it: sexcapades.io/#app/import. To import: open the link on another phone → age gate → Scan or Import dialog.

Compare hypermaps?

The hook. The question you ask someone when you want to compare profiles. Not "compare kink maps."

Kink categories

The first-generation lines radiating from the center node. Each represents one of the 18 top-level kink categories (e.g., Power exchange, Service & ritual). Tapping a category fans out its kink lines.

Kink lines

The second-generation lines radiating from a kink category. Each represents a specific kink or practice. The line is split: the top half shows the give rating, the bottom half shows the receive rating, with the hanky-code color in the center.

Veil

The Intimacy Veil — the visibility filter. A slider from Revealed to Concealed that controls which of your ratings show on the map, so you can share a map without showing everything. Changing the veil only filters what is visible; it never changes your ratings. Previously called Net.

Group profile

A combined profile minted from two or more selected profiles, showing where they overlap, their mismatches, and their blind spots. It is minted once and stands on its own — changing a member profile later does not change it. Select the tabs, and the group appears as a new tab.

TaxoPolemics

The engine underneath the maps. TaxoPolemics turns a hard conversation into a hypermap: it counts the poles — the stances a topic can take, up to eight — and lets you rate how much each stance matters to you. One engine, fifteen hypermaps. The app you use is Flexcapades; the engine is TaxoPolemics.

Indaba

In isiZulu and isiXhosa, a matter of importance — and the gathering called to talk it through, until everyone has been heard and a common mind emerges. The engine treats a hard talk as an indaba, not a debate: the point is not to win it, but to hear what everyone believes. See TaxoPolemics.

Skins

Fifteen hypermaps, one engine. Each skin is the same TaxoPolemics engine pointed at a different hard conversation. Sexcapades (kink & sexuality, 18+), Poliscapades (power and the vote), Forescapades (Abrahamic faith), Philoscapades (how we know), Aesthcapades (beauty), Vocapades (work and worth), Techcapades (what we build), Dharmacapades (how we walk the earth), Theoricapades (the big picture), Praxicapades (what we do), Quantacapades (the small and strange), Expericapades (how experiments are done), Simulcapades (copies and what’s real), Mattercapades (stuff and energy), and Chemcapades (how molecules combine). Each lives at its own address: <prefix>.capades.io — for example, polis.capades.io. See The picker.

Depth Factor

The vanilla name for Kink Factor. On the vanilla skins, the pair number — how deep two people’s positions run together — is called Depth Factor, because those maps measure depth of knowledge or stance, not kink. On Sexcapades it is still called Kink Factor. See Kink Factor, Crossmatch.

Crossmatch

The vanilla name for Krossmatch. The full pair grid — every shared topic, both directions — is called Crossmatch on the vanilla skins and Krossmatch on Sexcapades. See Krossmatch, Depth Factor.

Kink Factor

The pair number on Sexcapades: how two profiles run together, contracted from the Krossmatch over their shared topics. On the vanilla skins it is called Depth Factor. See Depth Factor.

Krossmatch

The full pair grid on Sexcapades — every shared topic, both directions, not a single number. On the vanilla skins it is called Crossmatch. See Crossmatch, Kink Factor.

Commune / Converse

The vanilla names for the pair modes. Play becomes Commune and Parlay becomes Converse on the vanilla skins. The pair scores follow: Play KF becomes Commune DF, Parlay KF becomes Converse DF, Play MS becomes Commune MS, and Parlay MS becomes Converse MS. See Depth Factor, Crossmatch.

Archetypes and Samples

Try a profile without rating everything yourself. Archetypes are standard positions the map was built from — a Sadist or a Rope Bunny on Sexcapades, a party platform on Poliscapades, a denominational creed on Forescapades. Samples are real people who shared their map — Adam Peasant, Eve Vale, and BothInches on Sexcapades. Pick one from the menu and the map fills with its ratings; compare it with yours like any profile.

The picker

Fifteen maps, one engine. The picker at flex.capades.io lists every hypermap — each card is the map’s full address, like polis.capades.io — with Sexcapades last, marked 18+. Pick a map to open it. See Skins.

The engine page

The page at capades.io about TaxoPolemics, the engine underneath the maps — the idea (an indaba, drawn as a map), the poles, what it measures, and the fifteen maps. See TaxoPolemics, Indaba.

Skin menu

The menu listing all fifteen skins. Open it by Shift+Clicking the header, or by pressing and holding the header for a moment. Pick a skin to switch maps; the checkmark sits on the map you are on. On flex.capades.io and capades.io the menu opens on its own, so you can pick a map first.

Hostname routing

The address picks the map. <prefix>.capades.io opens the app on that skin; flex.capades.io opens the picker; capades.io opens the engine page; sex.capades.io (and sexcapades.io) open the age gate first. You can also add ?skin=<skin> to any app address to open that skin directly.
