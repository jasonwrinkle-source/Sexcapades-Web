Hypermap

Your visual profile — a radial map of 404 kink topics, color-coded by your give/receive interest. Not a "kink map."

Quick Start

Rate 3 topics per category (54 total) in ~20 minutes. Gives you a partial hypermap you can already compare or turn into a Scribe story. The full 404 topics are optional.

Net

Visibility filter (button underneath the camera icon). Controls which kink ratings are visible on the map — filter by interest level to declutter the view.

Cast

Compatibility strictness control (fishing reel icon underneath the + sign, adjacent to the profile tabs). Controls the degree of mutual compatibility allowed when multi-selecting profiles for grouping, before it creates a new combined profile tab (the vector cross product of the multi-selected profiles). Wider cast = more lenient matching; narrower cast = stricter.

Scribe

Story generator. Turn your hypermap into an erotic story (single profile) or a play guide (multiple profiles). Works with a partial Quick Start map — no partner required.

Scribe vs Scene Setter: Scribe writes scenes and stories (creative). Scene Setter plans and negotiates play (practical). Use Scribe for fantasy, Scene Setter for real-world coordination.

Scene Setter

Play planner. Plan and negotiate a scene with a partner — boundaries, safewords, aftercare. Practical coordination, not storytelling. Pair with Scribe: Scene Setter plans it, Scribe writes it.

Scan or Import

Previously called "Scan a Sync Card."

The dialog for importing profiles. Three ways in: (1) scan a QR code with your camera, (2) upload a JPEG/PNG image of a QR code, (3) upload an exported file (PNG, SVG, PDF, sync card). JSON files are also accepted.

Sync Card

Business-card-size (3.5"×2") physical card with your profile QR codes. Front has a QR linking to the scanner; back has your profile data as numbered QR codes. Print and hand out at munches.

QR Sheet

Not "QR page."

A page of QR codes (black on white, one per image) for sharing in online galleries or picture sets. Each sheet has the deep link printed on it: sexcapades.io/#app/import. To import: open the link on another phone → age gate → Scan or Import dialog.

Compare hypermaps?

The hook. The question you ask someone when you want to compare profiles. Not "compare kink maps."

Kink categories

The first-generation lines radiating from the center node. Each represents one of the 18 top-level kink categories (e.g., Power exchange, Service & ritual). Tapping a category fans out its kink lines.

Kink lines

The second-generation lines radiating from a kink category. Each represents a specific kink or practice. The line is split: the top half shows the give rating, the bottom half shows the receive rating, with the hanky-code color in the center.
