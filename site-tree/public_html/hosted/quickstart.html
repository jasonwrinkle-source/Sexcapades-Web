Quick start

Find what clicks—in about 20 minutes.

Sexcapades maps ~400 kinks. You rate each one for giving and for receiving; when a partner rates theirs, the app overlays your two maps and colors every topic by how you match.

Three steps

~20 minutes

01

Open the Hot list

The list is a three-level outline. Indentation shows the hierarchy: categories sit at the left edge, subcategories indent one level, and individual kinks indent furthest. At every level, items are ordered by popularity — the most-rated category, subcategory, or kink comes first.

7 of the 18 categories split into subcategories (44 in total). A › beside a name means it is collapsed; tap it and the arrow turns down (⌄) as its contents expand in place.

Already have a sync card? Use Scan in the app to import it — from the physical card or a QR photo someone sends you. You can also upload any exported file directly (SVG, PNG, PDF, or sync card).

Go to the Hot list →
02

Rate 3 topics per category

Every topic has two sliders: give sits above the topic’s line, receive below it. Drag either one to set how much you want that side of it — the scale runs from Not Interested through Curious about to Want to do, and you can also flag a topic Hard Limit or Fantasy-only.

  • Each category starts by showing only its 3 most popular kinks.
  • Tap “Show the next three popular” to reveal 3 more. The ladder climbs 3 → 6 → 9 → 12 → 15 → 18 → 21 per category, and subcategories appear in place as the ladder reaches them.
  • Stopping at 3 per category covers all 18 categories — 54 topics in about 20 minutes.
03

Compare with a partner

Import a partner’s hypermap (step 1) and the app combines both profiles into one: each cross-pairing is scored by fit — your give against their receive, and their give against your receive — and colored by the result. Solo? Scribe generates stories and play guides from just your profile.

When you’re done rating, use the single centered button in the header to save: with no profile loaded it reads “Create profile from ratings”; with a profile loaded it reads “Submit what you’ve got” and saves edits to that same profile.

Good to know

  • Each extra rung adds 3 topics to every category: 6 per category is 108 topics (~40 min), 9 is 162 (~1 hr), 12 is 216 (~80 min), 15 is 270 (~100 min), 18 is 324 (~2 hrs), 21 is 378 (~2 hrs 20 min). Past 21, the full ~400-topic map takes about 2.5 hours.
  • New in 3.5: touch targets are now a comfortable 44px and dialogs are cleaner throughout.

What you get

Your saved ratings feed two tools: Scribe turns a profile into a story or scene prompt, and Scene Setter turns two profiles into a plan for negotiating play. Scene Sentinel adds check-in timers and emergency contacts to help keep play safer.

Take your results with you.

  • SVG (recommended) — preferred for a crisp, scalable map that matches the app viewport.
  • PNG — best for sharing online and archiving; your profile is embedded, so keeping the PNG keeps your profile.
  • PDF — best for printing or sharing as a fixed document.
  • Sync cards — best for handing to someone in person; they scan the QR with the app to import your profile.
  • Important: Chat apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, etc.) strip a PNG’s embedded profile data when it’s sent as a photo. To share an importable profile, send the actual file by email attachment, file share, or AirDrop—not as a chat-app image.

Privacy

Your map stays with you.

No account. No cloud. Your data never leaves your device. Your profile is exported in one of several formats when you choose to share.
