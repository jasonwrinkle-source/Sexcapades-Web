*Taxopolemics Engine Specification Briefing for Autonomous Agents & Modelers*

**Engine:** Taxopolemics (Multi-Pole Dialectical Hypermap Engine)  
**Skin:** Techcapades (Technology, AI, Privacy & Digital Life)

## 1. Abstract & Scope

**Techcapades** maps human positions on technology and digital life — what you think of AI, how you guard your privacy, how much screen you live behind, how you connect online, who you are when you are digital, and what you think the machines are doing to us. In traditional surveys, tech attitudes are collapsed into crude single-axis binaries (e.g. Pro-Tech vs. Anti-Tech; Online vs. Offline) or reduced to a list of apps you use.

Under the **Taxopolemics** engine, tech positions are mapped to their natural geometric degrees of freedom: from **1-Pole scalars** (absolute affinity), to **2-Pole dialectical dipoles** (Privacy vs. Convenience; Accelerate vs. Decelerate), to higher-order **Triads (3-Pole $\Delta^2$)**, **Tetrads (4-Pole $\Delta^3$)**, **Pentads (5-Pole $\Delta^4$)**, **Hexads (6-Pole $\Delta^5$)**, **Heptads (7-Pole $\Delta^6$)**, and **Octads (8-Pole $\Delta^7$)**.

**Engine cap:** the engine goes to **8 poles and caps there** — monopole through octad. The cap is a human limit, not a technical one: people cannot hold more than about eight positions in mind at once (Jason, 2026-11-14).

**Scope note:** Techcapades is the seventh skin. It covers technology and digital life — where people have real positions, those positions are live and contested, and comparing profiles is about compatibility (can we share a screen, a home, a life, when we disagree about the machines?). The ground shifts fast here — a taxonomy of AI attitudes from 2026 may look dated quickly — so the topics are framed as stances, not as product preferences. Techcapades brushes Philoscapades (transhumanism, AI consciousness) and Poliscapades (tech regulation, AI policy, privacy law); the difference is angle. Techcapades asks how *you* live with the machines. Philoscapades asks what the machines *are*. Poliscapades asks what the law *should do* about them.

## 2. Taxonomic Architecture & Rating Widget Assignments

### Category 1: AI & Intelligence

#### 1.1 AI Risk & Benefit Allocation (5-Pole Pentad $\Delta^4$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Green #22c55e)`: **Post-Scarcity Flourishing** — Radical abundance, medicine, automation dividends.
  - `Pole 1 (Yellow #eab308)`: **Corporate / Algorithmic Feudalism** — Concentration of capital, surveillance, platform monopoly.
  - `Pole 2 (Orange #f97316)`: **Labor & Cognitive Atrophy** — Erosion of human skills, mass displacement, cultural stagnation.
  - `Pole 3 (Red #ef4444)`: **Existential Misalignment** — Loss of agency, superintelligent runaway, extinction.
  - `Pole 4 (Blue #3b82f6)`: **Epistemic Fragmentation** — Synthetic deepfakes, death of objective consensus, dead internet.
- **Question:** Where do you see AI's impact landing?
- **Optimal Widget:** Pentad radial star.
- **Note:** Replaces the earlier Utopian→Doomer pentad, which was a 1-dimensional sentiment slider disguised as a 5-simplex (an individual cannot coherently be 80% Utopian AND 80% Doomer simultaneously). The new poles are distinct vectors of impact, not points on a line.

#### 1.2 AI as Tool vs Agent (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Tool (AI does what you tell it) ↔ Agent (AI acts on its own)
- **Question:** How much autonomy should AI have?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 1.3 Open Weights vs Closed Moats (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Open Source / Decentralized (Democratized weights, permissionless innovation, public inspection) ↔ Guarded Moats / Frontier Containment (Closed APIs, non-proliferation, centralized safety oversight)
- **Question:** Should AI weights be open or guarded?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

### Category 2: Privacy & Surveillance

#### 2.1 Privacy vs Convenience (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Privacy (protect my data) ↔ Convenience (use my data to make things easy)
- **Question:** The classic tradeoff — what do you give up, and what do you get?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 2.2 Trust vs Distrust Big Tech (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Trust (they mostly mean well) ↔ Distrust (they mostly extract)
- **Question:** Do you trust the companies that hold your data?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 2.3 Safety vs Freedom (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Safety (surveillance keeps us safe) ↔ Freedom (surveillance threatens liberty)
- **Question:** When safety and freedom conflict, which wins?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 2.4 Computing Sovereignty (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Sovereign Computing (Self-hosting, local hardware, Linux, Right to Repair, data ownership) ↔ Managed Cloud (Apple/Google ecosystem, seamless subscription convenience, zero maintenance)
- **Question:** Do you own your computing or rent it?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

### Category 3: Screens & Attention

#### 3.1 Screen-light vs Screen-heavy (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Screen-light (minimal, deliberate) ↔ Screen-heavy (always connected)
- **Question:** How much of your day lives behind a screen?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 3.2 Notifications On vs Off (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** All on (interrupt me) ↔ All off (silent, check on my terms)
- **Question:** How interruptible are you?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 3.3 Digital Detox (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **Detox regularly** — Scheduled breaks, digital sabbaths, the phone goes away.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Detox occasionally** — When it gets bad, step back for a while.
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Never detox** — No need; the screen is fine, leave me alone.
- **Question:** Do you step away from the screen, and how often?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget.

### Category 4: Connection & Social Media

#### 4.1 Social Media (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **Love** — It connects us, it is where people are.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Tolerate** — It is useful and awful at the same time.
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Hate** — It poisons us; delete it all.
- **Question:** What do you think of social media?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget.

#### 4.2 Online vs In-person (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Online connection ↔ In-person connection
- **Question:** Where do your real relationships live?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 4.3 Posting vs Lurking (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Poster (share, create, speak) ↔ Lurker (consume, observe, stay quiet)
- **Question:** How do you participate online?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 4.4 Synthetic Intimacy & AI Companionship (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Embrace Synthetic Bonds (AI partners, parasocial companions, synthetic therapy, digital confidants) ↔ Human Exclusivity (Strict boundary; intimacy and emotional trust reserved for biological humans)
- **Question:** Are you open to emotional or intimate connections with synthetic agents?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

### Category 5: Digital Identity & Self

#### 5.1 Anonymity vs Real Name (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Anonymous (pseudonym, no real name) ↔ Real identity (your name, your face)
- **Question:** Do you use your real name online?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 5.2 Curated vs Authentic Self (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Curated (polished, managed, the highlight reel) ↔ Authentic (raw, unfiltered, the real thing)
- **Question:** How do you present yourself online?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 5.3 Erase vs Archive (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Erase (delete it all when you go) ↔ Archive (preserve it, let it stand)
- **Question:** What happens to your data when you die?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

### Category 6: The Machine Future

#### 6.1 Automation: Job Loss vs Liberation (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Job loss (automation destroys work) ↔ Liberation (automation frees us from work)
- **Question:** Does automation take your job, or give you your life back?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 6.2 Transhumanism (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **Embrace enhancement** — Merge with the machines; upgrade the body, extend the mind.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Cautious augmentation** — Some enhancement, carefully, with limits.
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Reject enhancement** — Stay human; the body is enough.
- **Question:** Would you enhance yourself with technology?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget.
- **Note:** Brushes Philoscapades. Techcapades treats transhumanism as a personal stance (would *you* enhance?); Philoscapades treats it as a philosophical position (is enhancement good?).

#### 6.3 Accelerate vs Decelerate (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Accelerate (move fast, build more, e/acc) ↔ Decelerate (slow down, be careful, pause)
- **Question:** Should we build faster, or pump the brakes?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

## 4. Higher-Pole Candidates (not yet specified)

- **Social Platforms** (pentad or hexad): X / Instagram / TikTok / Reddit / YouTube (+ Facebook). Platforms are a preference list, not a pole structure, and they change fast — may be better as multiple 1-pole topics, or as a platform map. Needs a ruling on whether platforms are positions or just habits.
- **Tech Ethics** (tetrad): Utilitarian / Deontological / Virtue / Care. Overlaps Philoscapades (normative ethics) — needs a ruling on whether it belongs here as applied ethics, or stays in philosophy.
- **AI Risk Positions** (pentad): Existential risk / Misalignment / Misuse / Concentration of power / Benign. A refinement of 1.1 for people who want more granularity on *how* AI goes wrong. Needs a ruling on whether it is distinct from 1.1, or whether 1.1 should expand.

## 5. Open Questions for the Principal Investigator

1. Is the scope right — technology and digital life as one skin, or should AI split off from screens/privacy?
2. Does 1.1 (AI Futures) stay a pentad, or should it expand to cover more positions (e.g. a hexad with accelerationism vs. degrowth)?
3. Are the dipole/triad assignments right, or do any of these want a different pole count?
4. Do the higher-pole candidates (platforms, tech ethics, AI risk positions) belong, or are they a different kind of thing?
5. The ground shifts fast here — is the stance framing (positions, not products) the right way to keep the taxonomy from dating itself, or does it need a different approach?
