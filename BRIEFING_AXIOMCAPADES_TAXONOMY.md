## 1. Abstract, Scope & Nomenclature

### 1.1 The Naming Dialectic
YoBiche proposed the name **Abstracapades** for pure mathematics. While the motivation (abstraction as the hallmark of pure math) is understandable, the name suffers from linguistic friction:
- *"Abstra-"* sounds clipped and phoneticly harsh when mated to *"-capades"*, risking confusion with "abstracted" (absent-minded) rather than mathematical abstraction.
- In contrast, pure mathematics is fundamentally defined by the **axiomatic method**—the derivation of eternal, deductive truth from unassailable foundational postulates, completely insulated from empirical contamination.

We propose **Axiomcapades** as the definitive flagship name:
1. **Axiomcapades**: Clean, dignified, mathematically pristine, and immediately evokes Euclidean rigor, Hilbertian formalization, and ZFC foundations.
2. **Theoricapades** (Strongest Classical Alternative): From Greek *theōria* (contemplative vision, intellectual viewing of divine truth), serving as the eternal dialectical twin to applied *praxis*.
3. **Formacapades / Eidoscapades**: Grounded in the Platonic realm of ideal mathematical forms.

### 1.2 Domain Scope
Pure mathematics is not merely "harder applied math" or "applied math without data." It is an autonomous metaphysical and aesthetic universe with its own distinct ontology, epistemology, sociology, and values:
- Its currency is **rigorous proof**, not predictive empirical fit.
- Its driving aesthetic is **structural elegance, unity, and depth**, not utility.
- Its central conflicts are philosophical (What is a mathematical object? What constitutes a valid proof?) and methodological (The hammer vs. the rising sea; concrete beauty vs. extreme abstraction).

Under the **Taxopolemics** engine, pure mathematical stances are mapped across their native geometries: from 1-pole scalars up to the strict human cognitive cap of **8-pole octads ($\Delta^7$)**.

## 2. Taxonomic Architecture & Rating Widget Assignments

### Category 1: Foundations & Mathematical Ontology (What is Math?)

#### 1.1 The Foundational Trilemma: Platonism vs. Formalism vs. Constructivism (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
*The eternal tripolar debate over the reality of mathematical objects.*
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex - Gold #eab308)`: **Platonism / Realism** — Mathematical objects (numbers, sets, manifolds) exist independently of human thought in an objective realm. Theorems are *discovered*, not invented.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right - Blue #3b82f6)`: **Formalism** — Mathematics is a formal game of manipulating meaningless symbols according to specified axiomatic rules (Hilbert). Truth is syntactic consistency, not metaphysical reality.
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left - Emerald #10b981)`: **Intuitionism / Constructivism** — Mathematics is an activity of the human mind (Brouwer, Bishop). An object exists *only* if an explicit algorithm or construction produces it.
- **Question:** What is the nature of mathematical truth?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget (`"triangle"`).

#### 1.2 Foundational Bedrock: Sets vs. Categories vs. Types (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
*The structural foundation upon which all mathematics should be erected.*
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Top)`: **ZFC Set Theory** — Classical cumulative hierarchy of sets ($V$). The universal standard since Cantor, Zermelo, and Fraenkel.
  - `Pole 1 (Right)`: **Category Theory / Topoi** — Arrows, functors, and universal properties (Mac Lane, Grothendieck). Structures and relations over elements.
  - `Pole 2 (Left)`: **Homotopy Type Theory (HoTT)** — Constructive Martin-Löf type theory with Voevodsky's Univalence Axiom. Identity is homotopy equivalence; natively machine-verifiable.
- **Question:** Where does mathematical structure ultimately reside?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget (`"triangle"`).

#### 1.3 The Law of Excluded Middle: Classical vs. Constructive Logic (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** **Classical Logic** (LEM holds: $P \lor \neg P$; double negation elimination; proof by contradiction is fully valid) ↔ **Constructive Logic** (LEM rejected; proof of $\exists x\, P(x)$ requires an explicit witness; contradiction only proves negation)
- **Question:** Is proof by contradiction genuine knowledge?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole Slider.

#### 1.4 Mathematical Universe Hypothesis: Realist Ontology (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** **Radical Mathematical Realism** (Tegmark: our physical universe literally *is* a mathematical structure) ↔ **Epistemic Anti-Realism** (Mathematics is a human cognitive language and mapping tool)
- **Question:** Is reality made of mathematics, or is mathematics made by human minds?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole Slider.

### Category 2: Method, Style & The Grothendieck Divide

#### 2.1 The Rising Sea vs. The Hammer & Chisel (2-Pole Dipole)
*Alexander Grothendieck's celebrated distinction in mathematical problem-solving.*
- **Poles:** **The Rising Sea** (Dissolve the problem: construct vast, conceptual categorical machinery until the theorem becomes completely obvious and sinks into the water without resistance) ↔ **The Hammer & Chisel** (The heroic direct assault: hard combinatorics, brutal inequalities, ad-hoc ingenuity, cracking the nut with raw force like Erdős)
- **Question:** Do you submerge problems in abstract machinery or crack them with direct ingenuity?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole Slider.

#### 2.2 Abstraction: Deep Generalization vs. Concrete Jewels (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** **Extreme Structural Abstraction** ($\infty$-categories, higher stacks, motives, universal properties; the search for the absolute meta-pattern) ↔ **Exceptional Concrete Jewels** (The Monster group, the Leech lattice, $E_8$, modular forms, special curves; deep love for unique idiosyncratic structures)
- **Question:** Are you captivated by universal abstractions or exceptional concrete gems?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole Slider.

#### 2.3 Erdős's "The Book": Proof Aesthetics (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
*What makes a mathematical proof truly supreme?*
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **Luminous Simplicity ("The Book")** — A proof straight from God's Book; two lines of breathtaking, unexpected ingenuity that make you gasp.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Architectural Monumentality** — A 1,000-page cathedral of structural logic (e.g. classification of finite simple groups, EGA/SGA, Wiles' proof of FLT).
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Constructive Computational Revelation** — An illuminating algorithm that explicitly calculates the inner mechanics.
- **Question:** What is the highest form of mathematical beauty in a proof?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget (`"triangle"`).

#### 2.4 Intuition: Geometric / Spatial vs. Algebraic / Symbolic (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** **Geometric / Spatial Intuition** (Visualizing manifolds, curvature, projections, topological flows) ↔ **Algebraic / Symbolic Rigor** (Symbolic transformation, exact commutations, formal syntactic structures)
- **Question:** Do you think in mental geometries or formal symbols?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole Slider.

### Category 3: The Four Pillars of Pure Mathematics

#### 3.1 The Quadrivium of Pure Mathematics (4-Pole Tetrad $\Delta^3$)
*The four sovereign kingdoms of pure mathematical thought.*
- **Poles:**
  - `North (#3b82f6 - Blue)`: **Algebra & Symmetry** — Groups, rings, fields, Galois theory, representation theory, Lie algebras. The study of symmetry, transformation, and structural operations.
  - `East (#10b981 - Green)`: **Geometry & Topology** — Differential geometry, Riemannian manifolds, algebraic topology, knot theory, symplectic geometry. The study of shape, space, and invariants under deformation.
  - `South (#ef4444 - Red)`: **Analysis & Continua** — Real/complex analysis, functional analysis, operator algebras, harmonic analysis, partial differential equations. The study of limits, infinity, measures, and continuous variation.
  - `West (#8b5cf6 - Purple)`: **Number Theory & Arithmetic** — Primes, modular forms, elliptic curves, algebraic number fields, L-functions, Diophantine equations. The Queen of Mathematics.
- **Question:** Where does your primary mathematical heart dwell?
- **Optimal Widget:** Orthogonal Diamond Deformation Polygon (`"diamond"`).

#### 3.2 Combinatorics & Discrete Structures (1-Pole Monopole)
- **Pole:** Affinity for Discrete Mathematics & Combinatorics (graphs, posets, extremal combinatorics, partition theory), 0–100.
- **Question:** How strong is your passion for the finite and discrete?

### Category 4: Epistemology & Verification

#### 4.1 Proof Verification: Computer Proof Assistants vs. Human Social Consensus (2-Pole Dipole)
*The modern revolution in the philosophy of mathematical certainty.*
- **Poles:** **Machine-Checked Formalization** (Lean, Coq, Isabelle; a theorem is not truly verified until type-checked to foundational kernel primitives) ↔ **Human Social Consensus** (Mathematical understanding is an ongoing dialogue among human minds; machine proofs miss semantic comprehension)
- **Question:** Does absolute certainty require interactive proof assistants?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole Slider.

#### 4.2 Axiom of Choice (AC) Stance (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
*The most controversial postulate in mathematical history.*
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Top)`: **Unconditional Acceptance (ZFC)** — Indispensable tool; without AC, functional analysis (Hahn-Banach, Tychonoff) collapses.
  - `Pole 1 (Right)`: **Skeptical Restriction (ZF + Dependent Choice)** — Accept weak choice for countable sequences, but reject arbitrary non-measurable sets (Banach-Tarski paradox is pathology).
  - `Pole 2 (Left)`: **Defiant Rejection (Constructive / Determinacy)** — Work in ZF + Axiom of Determinacy (AD), where every set of reals is Lebesgue measurable.
- **Question:** How do you relate to the Axiom of Choice?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget (`"triangle"`).

#### 4.3 Conjecture Orientation: Conjecture-Solver vs. Theory-Builder (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** **Conjecture Solver** (Hunts famous white whales: Riemann Hypothesis, BSD, Goldbach; fame comes from conquering open problems) ↔ **Theory Builder** (Develops deep new conceptual languages, frameworks, and theories where old problems dissolve)
- **Question:** Do you slay famous open problems or build new mathematical worlds?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole Slider.

### Category 5: Relations to Reality & The World

#### 5.1 G.H. Hardy's Stance: Proud Inutility vs. Unreasonable Effectiveness (2-Pole Dipole)
*The pure mathematician's relationship to physical reality.*
- **Poles:** **Proud Inutility (Hardy's Apology)** (Pure math is noble, innocent, and beautiful precisely because it has no base military or commercial applications) ↔ **Unreasonable Effectiveness (Wigner)** (Awe and reverence that esoteric pure mathematics consistently and mysteriously anticipates the fundamental laws of nature)
- **Question:** Is pure math untainted because it has no application, or miraculous because it secretly mirrors physics?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole Slider.

#### 5.2 Pure Mathematics vs. Physics Boundary (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** **Autonomous Self-Reference** (Mathematics answers only to its own internal logic and aesthetic consistency) ↔ **Physical Symbiosis** (Deep mathematical breakthroughs are fertilized by quantum field theory, string theory, and general relativity)
- **Question:** Should pure math be guided by internal consistency or physical insight?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole Slider.
