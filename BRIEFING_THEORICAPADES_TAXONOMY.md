## 1. Abstract & Scope

**Theoricapades** maps human positions on pure and theoretical mathematics — what math is, what makes it true, how it is proved, what makes it beautiful, which branch is home, and how you live as a mathematician. In traditional surveys, mathematical attitudes are collapsed into crude single-axis binaries (e.g. Good at math vs. Bad at math) or reduced to a list of courses taken.

Under the **Taxopolemics** engine, mathematical positions are mapped to their natural geometric degrees of freedom: from **1-Pole scalars** (absolute affinity), to **2-Pole dialectical dipoles** (Constructive vs. Classical; Rigor vs. Intuition), to higher-order **Triads (3-Pole $\Delta^2$)**, **Tetrads (4-Pole $\Delta^3$)**, **Pentads (5-Pole $\Delta^4$)**, **Hexads (6-Pole $\Delta^5$)**, **Heptads (7-Pole $\Delta^6$)**, and **Octads (8-Pole $\Delta^7$)**.

**Engine cap:** the engine goes to **8 poles and caps there** — monopole through octad. The cap is a human limit, not a technical one: people cannot hold more than about eight positions in mind at once (Jason, 2026-11-14).

**Scope note:** Theoricapades is the pure and theoretical mathematics skin, paired with **Praxicapades** (applied mathematics) — the two are different cultures with different values, and a single skin would dilute both. The pure/applied divide is itself a topic in this skin (3.4). Theoricapades brushes Philoscapades (philosophy of mathematics — Platonism, formalism, and the nature of mathematical truth live in epistemology and metaphysics); this skin goes deeper into the specifically mathematical positions, the way Dharmacapades goes deeper than Forescapades. The audience is narrower than the other skins — mathematicians, physicists, and math-adjacent people — but the positions are real and passionate, and for collaborators and co-authors they determine whether you can work together.

**Name note:** The name is **Theoricapades**, from Greek *theōria* (contemplative vision, intellectual viewing of divine truth) — the Principal Investigator's pick. It forms the classical theoria/praxis pair with **Praxicapades** (applied math), the eternal dialectical twin of theory and practice. It supersedes **Abstracapades** (YoBiche's first draft — Jason picked it over Axiomacapades, then changed his mind) and **Axiomcapades** (Gemini's flagship name, with Theoricapades as her strongest classical alternative). Gemini's critique of Abstracapades: "Abstra-" sounds clipped and harsh when mated to "-capades," risking confusion with "abstracted" (absent-minded) rather than mathematical abstraction.

**Synthesis note:** This briefing merges YoBiche's Abstracapades (18 topics — broader, more accessible, covering beauty, the practical mathematical life, and the general attitudes) with Gemini's Axiomcapades (18 topics — deeper, more technically specific, naming the theorems, the people, and the canonical results). Neither version alone is complete; together they are strong. Where both sources cover the same topic, the better version was kept, as noted per topic.

#### 1.2 Foundational Bedrock: Sets vs. Categories vs. Types (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
*The structural foundation upon which all mathematics should be erected.*
- **Poles:**

  - `Pole 1 (Right)`: **Category Theory / Topoi** — Arrows, functors, and universal properties (Mac Lane, Grothendieck). Structures and relations over elements.
  - `Pole 2 (Left)`: **Homotopy Type Theory (HoTT)** — Constructive Martin-Löf type theory with Voevodsky's Univalence Axiom. Identity is homotopy equivalence; natively machine-verifiable.
- **Question:** Where does mathematical structure ultimately reside?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget (`"triangle"`).
- **Source:** Axiomcapades 1.2 (Abstracapades did not cover this).

- **Hovertip:** **Foundational Bedrock: Sets vs. Categories vs. Types** [NON-BLEND]

- **Question:** Is proof by contradiction genuine knowledge?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole Slider.
- **Source:** Axiomcapades 1.3 (the LEM detail; Abstracapades 2.1 is the same topic in plainer form).

- **Hovertip:** **The Law of Excluded Middle: Classical vs. Constructive Logic**

#### 4.3 Axiom of Choice (AC) Stance (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
*The most controversial postulate in mathematical history.*
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Top)`: **Unconditional Acceptance (ZFC)** — Indispensable tool; without AC, functional analysis (Hahn-Banach, Tychonoff) collapses.
  - `Pole 1 (Right)`: **Skeptical Restriction (ZF + Dependent Choice)** — Accept weak choice for countable sequences, but reject arbitrary non-measurable sets (Banach-Tarski paradox is pathology).
  - `Pole 2 (Left)`: **Defiant Rejection (Constructive / Determinacy)** — Work in ZF + Axiom of Determinacy (AD), where every set of reals is Lebesgue measurable.
- **Question:** How do you relate to the Axiom of Choice?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget (`"triangle"`).
- **Source:** Axiomcapades 4.2.

- **Hovertip:** **Axiom of Choice (AC) Stance** [LINE]
  - Issue: How do you relate to the Axiom of Choice?
  - Unconditional Acceptance (ZFC) — Indispensable; without AC, functional analysis collapses.
  - Skeptical Restriction (ZF + Dependent Choice) — Accept weak choice; reject non-measurable sets.
  - Defiant Rejection (ZF + AD) — Every set of reals is Lebesgue measurable.
