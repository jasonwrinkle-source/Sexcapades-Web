## 1. Abstract & Scope

**Vocapades** maps human positions on work and vocation — why you work, how hard you push, where and how you work, how work is organized, what you get for it, and what work means to who you are. In traditional surveys, work attitudes are collapsed into crude single-axis binaries (e.g. Ambitious vs. Lazy; Career vs. Family) or reduced to a job title.

Under the **Taxopolemics** engine, work positions are mapped to their natural geometric degrees of freedom: from **1-Pole scalars** (absolute affinity), to **2-Pole dialectical dipoles** (Purpose vs. Paycheck; Hustle vs. Balance), to higher-order **Triads (3-Pole $\Delta^2$)**, **Tetrads (4-Pole $\Delta^3$)**, **Pentads (5-Pole $\Delta^4$)**, **Hexads (6-Pole $\Delta^5$)**, **Heptads (7-Pole $\Delta^6$)**, and **Octads (8-Pole $\Delta^7$)**.

**Engine cap:** the engine goes to **8 poles and caps there** — monopole through octad. The cap is a human limit, not a technical one: people cannot hold more than about eight positions in mind at once (Jason, 2026-11-14).

**Scope note:** Vocapades is the sixth skin. It covers work and vocation — where people have real positions, those positions shape relationships profoundly, and comparing profiles is about compatibility (can we build a life together when we disagree about what work is for?). Vocation is about calling and purpose, not just clocking in — that is why the skin is Vocapades, not Workcapades (Jason, 2026-11-14). Work sits close to money, which was ruled out as a skin; but work is about more than money — it is about purpose and how you spend your days.

## 2. Taxonomic Architecture & Rating Widget Assignments

### Category 1: Purpose & Meaning

#### 1.1 Vocation: Calling vs Career vs Job (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **Calling** — Work is an inseparable expression of moral purpose, identity, and personal mission. Fulfillment is found in the contribution itself.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Career** — Work is a structured ladder of advancement, status, leadership, professional mastery, and upward social mobility.
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Job** — Work is a pragmatic economic transaction. A paycheck buys freedom, safety, and leisure; real life happens off the clock.
- **Question:** Is your work a calling, a career, or a job?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget.
- **Note:** Based on Amy Wrzesniewski's canonical research (Yale) — work orientation is fundamentally an irreducible triad, not two separate dipoles. Replaces the earlier 1.1 (Purpose vs Paycheck) and 1.2 (Craft vs Career).

#### 1.2 Service: Others vs Self vs The Work (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **Serve Others** — The work matters because it helps people.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Serve Yourself** — The work matters because it fulfills you.
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Serve The Work** — The work matters in itself; the craft is the point.
- **Question:** Who is the work for?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget.

### Category 2: Effort & Ambition

#### 2.1 Hustle vs Balance (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Hustle / Grind ↔ Balance / Rest
- **Question:** Do you push, or do you pace yourself? The big one.
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 2.2 Content vs Ambitious (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Contentment (enough is enough) ↔ Ambition (always more)
- **Question:** Are you satisfied where you are, or always reaching?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 2.3 Risk Tolerance (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Safe / Stable ↔ Risky / Entrepreneurial
- **Question:** Do you want a steady job, or a bet?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 2.4 Generalist vs Specialist (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Polymath / Generalist (Wide breadth, multidisciplinary synthesis, connecting dots across fields) ↔ Deep Specialist (Vertical mastery, single-domain depth, world-class technical precision)
- **Question:** Are you a generalist or a specialist?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

### Category 3: Place & Mode

#### 3.1 Remote vs Hybrid vs Office (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **Remote** — Work from anywhere, async by default.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Hybrid** — Some days in, some days out.
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Office** — Show up, be present, the room matters.
- **Question:** Where do you do your best work?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget.

#### 3.2 Solo vs Team (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Solo / Independent ↔ Team / Collaborative
- **Question:** Do you work best alone, or with others?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 3.3 Structured vs Flexible Hours (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Fixed schedule ↔ Flexible / Async
- **Question:** When do you work — on the clock, or on your own rhythm?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

### Category 4: Structure & Authority

#### 4.1 Hierarchy vs Flat (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Hierarchical (clear chain of command) ↔ Flat (egalitarian, no bosses)
- **Question:** How should work be organized?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 4.2 Autonomy vs Direction (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Autonomy (decide for yourself) ↔ Direction (be told what to do)
- **Question:** How much freedom do you want in how you work?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 4.3 Management Philosophy (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **Command** — Top-down, clear orders, the boss decides.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Coach** — Supportive, developmental, the manager grows the team.
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Laissez-faire** — Hands-off, get out of the way, trust the people.
- **Question:** How should a manager manage?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget.

#### 4.4 Workplace Governance & Ownership (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Corporate / Capital Hierarchy (Investor-owned, executive command, market efficiency) ↔ Worker Cooperative / Collective (Employee ownership, profit-sharing, democratic workplace decisions)
- **Question:** Who should own the workplace?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 4.5 Work Models (5-Pole Pentad $\Delta^4$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Blue #3b82f6)`: **Full-time Employee** — One employer, steady role.
  - `Pole 1 (Purple #a855f7)`: **Freelancer / Independent** — Clients, not bosses.
  - `Pole 2 (Orange #f97316)`: **Founder / Entrepreneur** — Build it yourself.
  - `Pole 3 (Teal #14b8a6)`: **Part-time / Portfolio** — Multiple part-time roles, a patchwork.
  - `Pole 4 (Green #22c55e)`: **Public Service / Nonprofit** — Mission over market.
- **Question:** What shape does your working life take?
- **Optimal Widget:** Pentad radial star.

### Category 5: Reward & Security

#### 5.1 Pay vs Perks (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** High pay ↔ Good perks / benefits
- **Question:** What matters more — the number, or the package?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 5.2 Security vs Upside (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Security (stable, predictable) ↔ Upside (equity, bonus, growth)
- **Question:** Do you want safety, or potential?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 5.3 Recognition (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Quiet contribution (the work speaks) ↔ Public recognition (need the credit)
- **Question:** How much do you need to be seen for what you do?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 5.4 The Four Career Anchors (4-Pole Tetrad $\Delta^3$)
- **Poles:**
  - `North (Green #22c55e)`: **Technical / Craft Mastery** — Deep competence in the work itself.
  - `East (Yellow #eab308)`: **Executive / Managerial Authority** — Steering organizations, accountability, power.
  - `South (Red #ef4444)`: **Autonomy / Creative Freedom** — Self-direction, working on one's own terms.
  - `West (Blue #3b82f6)`: **Security / Institutional Stability** — Predictability, safety, reliable long-term foundation.
- **Question:** What anchors your career?
- **Optimal Widget:** Orthogonal Diamond Deformation Polygon.
- **Note:** Derived from Edgar Schein's career anchor research.

### Category 6: Identity & Boundaries

#### 6.1 Work as Identity (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Job is who I am ↔ Job is what I do
- **Question:** Is your work your identity, or just something you do?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 6.2 Boundaries (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Permeable (work bleeds into life) ↔ Firm (clear separation)
- **Question:** How separate is work from life — and should it be?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 6.3 Retirement (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Retire (stop when you can) ↔ Work for life (never stop)
- **Question:** Do you work to retire, or do you work because the work is the point?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

## 4. Higher-Pole Candidates (not yet specified)

- **Industries** (pentad or hexad): Tech / Healthcare / Creative / Trades / Public sector (+ Finance). Industries are more of a preference list than a pole structure — may be better as multiple 1-pole topics, or as a sector map.
- **Leadership Styles** (tetrad): Autocratic / Democratic / Servant / Transformational. Overlaps 4.3 (Management Philosophy) — needs a ruling on whether it is distinct, or whether 4.3 should expand to a tetrad.
- **Career Stages** (triad): Building (early, hungry) / Sustaining (mid, steady) / Winding down (late, legacy). A life-stage axis, not a position — needs a ruling on whether it belongs in the taxonomy at all.
