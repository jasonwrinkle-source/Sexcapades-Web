## 1. Abstract & Scope

**Forescapades** adapts the **Taxopolemics** engine to the domain of the sacred, metaphysical ontology, theology, mystical experience, and contemplative discipline.

Historically, religious and spiritual typologies have been paralyzed by dogmatic sectarian binaries (Believer vs. Infidel; Sacred vs. Profane; Theist vs. Atheist). In reality, spiritual orientations and metaphysical worldviews inhabit a rich, multi-dimensional geometric continuum spanning simplices $\Delta^{n-1}$ from $n=1$ to $n=7$. Forescapades gives practitioners, philosophers of religion, and spiritual seekers an objective, non-sectarian geometric language to express existential orientation, contemplative practice, and cosmological models.
