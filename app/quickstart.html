Climb the phases at your pace

Every topic has two sliders: give sits above the topic’s line, receive below it. Drag either one to set how much you want that side of it — the scale runs from Not Interested through Curious about to Want to do, and you can also flag a topic Hard Limit or Fantasy-only.

Every topic presents its rating sliders or polygon canvas. Drag to set your position and receptivity across each pole or dimension — the scale runs from Not Interested through Curious about to Strongly aligned, and you can also flag Hard Limits or nuanced preferences.

Compare with a partner

Import a partner’s hypermap (step 1) and the app combines both profiles into one: each cross-pairing is scored by fit — your give against their receive, and their give against your receive — and colored by the result. Solo? Scribe generates stories and play guides from just your profile.

How Guided Ladder orders topics

  1. 01
    Greedy orthogonal first ladder — separates every headline archetype from every other in the fewest ratings.
  2. 02
    Branch round-robin pole splits — balances every category evenly (to 3/cat, then 6/cat), drawing each branch’s sharpest archetype fault lines first.
  3. 03
    Popularity — remaining topics ordered globally by community rating volume so widely relevant ones surface first.
  4. 04
    Alphabetical — deterministic tie-breaker for the long tail so rows never jump while you rate.

Take your results with you.

  • .CapadesIO (recommended) — preferred for sharing, waving, and backing up your profile; compact JSON data with no scripts, safe to open anywhere.
  • PNG — optional visual snapshot to show your map to a room or archive; your profile is embedded, so keeping the original PNG keeps your profile.
  • SVG / SVGZ — interactive scalable map with hover tooltips that matches the app viewport.
  • PDF — best for printing or sharing as a fixed document.
  • Sync cards — best for handing to someone in person; they scan the QR with the app to import your profile.
  • Important: Chat apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, etc.) strip a PNG’s embedded profile data when it’s sent as a photo. To share an importable profile, send your .CapadesIO file (or send the original export as a file attachment, not as a chat-app photo).
