    How FetLife import works
    1. Save your FetLife profile page with your browser’s “Save Page As” (HTML). Before saving, click every “View all” link in your Fetishes section — there are usually 2 (Into and Curious), sometimes 3 or more if you’ve entered hard limits or other categories. Saving without opening them truncates the lists and the import will miss fetishes.
    2. Upload the saved file — or paste the HTML — into the importer.
    3. The importer extracts the Fetishes section: the “Into” and “Curious about” lists.
    4. Each fetish is looked up in the importer’s mapping table.
    5. “Into” fetishes are mapped to topics with “interested” ratings (green).
    6. “Curious about” fetishes are mapped with “curious / neutral” ratings (yellow).
    7. Fetishes with no mapping go to an “unmapped” list shown with a warning — not silently dropped.
    8. If a “View all” link remains inside the Fetishes section, a truncation warning appears (the list may be incomplete). “View all” links elsewhere on the page are ignored.
    9. Giving and receiving contexts set the matching side of a rating. “Everything to do with it” rates 80% (4/5) in Into and about a third (deep yellow) in Curious about — on a one-slider topic it sets that single slider; on a two-slider topic it sets both give and receive. Watching and wearing contexts become topic annotations, worded with the topic’s own role labels where it has them, instead of being forced into give/receive.
    10. Favorites map to Favorite; Not into and soft limits map to Not Interested (soft limits also keep a note); hard limits map to Hard Limit. These discrete labels are never converted into slider positions.
    11. Comma-separated names and rows such as Fetish, Into, Giving are supported. A manual slider rating changes only when an import moves the topic to a different camp — for, curious, or against; differences within the same camp leave it untouched.
    12. Limitations: the mapping tables were built quickly and are low-confidence; some fetishes won’t map to existing topics and the table may not reflect the latest FetLife categories. Always review the results.
