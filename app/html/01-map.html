    Category & subcategory heat
    The colors above rate single topics. A category or subcategory instead takes the average of every rating inside it, and its line and icon wear that average as a heat color:
    Green-leaning — the topics rated inside mostly say Hot topics
    Yellow — the inside ratings are mixed, or mostly Curiosity?
    Red-leaning — the topics rated inside mostly say Not topics

    The stronger the average, the deeper the color. Favorites pull a branch toward green; Hard Limits and Not Interested pull it toward red. A branch with nothing rated inside yet keeps its plain neutral color — no heat until something inside it is rated.

    Show all terms
    Hypermap — Your personal kink mind map showing give/receive ratings across categories.
    Kink categories — The first-generation lines radiating from the center node (18 top-level categories).
    Kink lines — The second-generation lines radiating from a category, each a specific kink. Top half = give, bottom half = receive.
    Quick Start — The 54-topic onboarding path (3 per category, ~18 min).
    Veil — Filter showing only topics rated by the selected profiles.
    Scribe — Writes scenes and stories from a profile’s ratings (creative writing).
    Scene Setter — Planning and negotiating play with a partner: a shared checklist and practical play guide.
    Scene Sentinel — The watch companion view (toolbar: Scribe / Scene Setter / Scene Sentinel, shown when two or more profiles are selected): safe signal, check-in timer, emergency contacts, pre- and post-scene check-ins, and the GibberLink watch link. Open it from the toolbar or the view menu.
    GibberLink — The phone↔watch link: short text payloads carried by ultrasound chirps (GGWave) or Bluetooth. The watch is identified by the profile KEY only — Eve's watch is “eve”, never a long profile slug — and the display name on the watch is the key.
    Watch setup (CFG) — “Set up watch” sends CFG:<key>:<timer> (e.g. CFG:eve:300) and waits for the watch to answer ACK:<key>:CFG. Until the ACK arrives the setup is not confirmed. The same setup is offered from a profile's Heat popover (“Set up watch for this profile”).
    Check-ins (CHK) — A targeted check-in sends CHK:<key>:P (pulse) or CHK:<key>:T (timer) to one watch. Broadcast sends CHK:ALL:P / CHK:ALL:T — the explicit ALL signifier — so every watch answers; older unkeyed incoming messages are still treated as broadcast and land on the Broadcast watch card.
    Heat on sync — Syncing a profile to its watch from the Heat popover also copies the profile's existing Heat to the watch (HEAT:<key>:<value>, −100 to +100) right after the setup, so the watch starts at the heat the profile already has.
    Bluetooth (BLE) — GibberLink's Bluetooth service is 0000FFF0-0000-1000-8000-00805F9B34FB, plain Just Works GATT, no pairing ceremony. Connect from the Sentinel; where Bluetooth is unavailable the ultrasound chirps carry the same messages automatically.
    GibberLink log — The Sentinel's OUT / IN log records every payload sent to and heard from a watch, with its transport (Bluetooth or ultrasound). Watch taps and readings arrive as <key>:TAP / DBL / HLD and <key>:E / H / N values.
    Hot List paths — Every topic in the Hot List and On Deck shows its full path (category code › subcategory › topic, e.g. CLOTHNG›Materials›Latex & rubber) under its name and in its hovertip.
