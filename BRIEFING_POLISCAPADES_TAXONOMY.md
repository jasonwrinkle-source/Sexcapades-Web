## 1. Abstract & Scope

**Poliscapades** applies the **Taxopolemics** multi-pole hypermap engine to civic engagement, political economy, institutional design, resource allocation, and ideological orientation.

Modern political discourse is severely degraded by 1-dimensional "Left vs. Right" binary reductions. In reality, political desires, civic priorities, and institutional allegiances form a high-dimensional state space. Poliscapades models these civic vectors across simplices $\Delta^{n-1}$ ranging from $n=1$ to $n=7$, enabling citizens, policy makers, and deliberative groups to map consensus, irreconcilable polarization, and coalition opportunities with geometric precision.

## 3. Detailed Higher-Pole Topics & Widget Assignments

### 3.1 3-Pole Triads ($\Delta^2$) — Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget

#### 3.1.1 Macro-Economic Structural Triad: State / Market / Commons
- **Poles**:
  - `Apex (Top)`: **The State (Command & Redistribution)** — Centralized public planning, progressive taxation, regulatory oversight, public utility monopolies.
  - `Base Right`: **The Market (Capital & Price Discovery)** — Private ownership, free enterprise, decentralized pricing, competitive capital allocation.
  - `Base Left`: **The Commons (Civil Society & Mutual Aid)** — Peer-to-peer open resource pools, worker cooperatives, community land trusts, decentralized autonomous organizations.
- **Optimal Widget**: **Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget** with center barycentric coordinate display and civic budget intensity slider.

#### 3.1.2 Rodrik's Political Trilemma of the World Economy
- **Poles**:
  - `Top`: **Hyperglobalization** — Frictionless international trade, capital mobility, uniform regulatory harmonization.
  - `Base Right`: **National Sovereignty** — Self-determination of nation-states, border integrity, domestic industrial policy.
  - `Base Left`: **Democratic Politics** — National electorate accountability, domestic labor protections, social welfare consensus.
- *Epistemic Note*: As established by Dani Rodrik, any society can choose at most two of the three simultaneously.
- **Optimal Widget**: Barycentric Triangle with trilemma perimeter constraint.

#### 3.1.3 Theories of Distributive Justice Triad
- **Poles**:
  - `Top`: **Need (Marxian / Welfare)** — Resources allocated according to human vulnerability and biological necessity.
  - `Base Right`: **Merit / Contribution (Productivist)** — Rewards scaled strictly to output, effort, talent, and market value.
  - `Base Left`: **Strict Equality (Egalitarian)** — Identical baseline per capita endowment regardless of contribution or condition.
- **Optimal Widget**: Barycentric Triangle.

### 3.2 4-Pole Tetrads ($\Delta^3$) — Orthogonal Diamond Deformation Polygon (Concept B-alt2)

#### 3.2.1 The Classic Political Compass Tetrad (`topic-political-compass`)
- **Poles**:
  - `North (Green #22c55e)`: **Authoritarian Left (State Socialism)** — Public command economy, heavy state apparatus, collective discipline (Marxist-Leninism, State Capitalism).
  - `East (Yellow #eab308)`: **Authoritarian Right (Traditionalist / Fascist)** — Private enterprise subordinated to national/cultural glory, strict social hierarchy, militarism.
  - `South (Red #ef4444)`: **Libertarian Right (Anarcho-Capitalism / Minarchism)** — Absolute private property, unregulated laissez-faire markets, abolition of state intrusion.
  - `West (Blue #3b82f6)`: **Libertarian Left (Democratic Confederalism / Anarcho-Syndicalism)** — Voluntary collectivism, direct democracy, worker-owned production, abolition of coercive hierarchy.
- **Optimal Widget**: **Orthogonal Diamond Deformation Polygon** (Concept B-alt2).

#### 3.2.2 Decision Ecology & Governance Modalities (`topic-decision-ecology`)
- **Poles**:
  - `North (Green)`: **Executive Mandate (Decisive Sovereign)** — Unitary executive leadership, rapid decision cycles in crises, sovereign emergency authority.
  - `East (Yellow)`: **Technocratic Meritocracy (Rule by Expertise)** — Bureaucratic civil service, algorithmic optimization, evidence-based policy insulated from populism.
  - `South (Red)`: **Direct Deliberative Democracy (Assembly)** — Citizens' assemblies, sortition, continuous referendum voting, participatory budgeting.
  - `West (Blue)`: **Consensus Federation (Veto / Unanimity)** — Strict consensus-seeking, minority veto power, decentralized local autonomy.
- **Optimal Widget**: Orthogonal Diamond Deformation Polygon.

#### 3.2.3 Fiscal & Monetary Policy Tetrad
- **Poles**:
  - `North (Green)`: **Modern Monetary Theory (MMT)** — Sovereign currency issuance to fund full employment; inflation managed via fiscal policy.
  - `East (Yellow)`: **Keynesian Counter-Cyclical Intervention** — Deficit spending during recessions, fiscal tightening during expansions.
  - `South (Red)`: **Sound Money / Monetarism** — Fixed supply or hard-money standards (Gold, Bitcoin, strict Taylor rule), private central bank independence.
  - `West (Blue)`: **Fiscal Austerity / Balanced Budget** — Constitutionally mandated debt ceilings, public expenditure reduction, minimal state borrowing.
- **Optimal Widget**: Orthogonal Diamond Deformation Polygon.

### 3.3 5-Pole Pentads ($\Delta^4$) — Regular Pentagram Multi-Affinity Widget

#### 3.3.1 Civic Resource Allocation Pentad (`topic-civic-allocation-pentad`)
A fundamental problem in public finance: how a society allocates its finite discretionary GDP across 5 vital civilizational priorities.
- **Poles**:
  - `Apex 0 ($90^\circ$, Top)`: **Healthcare & Social Welfare** — Universal health systems, pensions, child allowances, disability support, homelessness prevention.
  - `Pole 1 ($18^\circ$, Upper Right)`: **Infrastructure & Clean Energy** — High-speed rail, smart grid, nuclear/solar power, water networks, transit.
  - `Pole 2 ($306^\circ$, Lower Right)`: **Education, Science & Frontier R&D** — Free public higher education, fundamental physics, fusion energy, space exploration.
  - `Pole 3 ($234^\circ$, Lower Left)`: **Defense, Border Security & Resilience** — Standing armed forces, intelligence, strategic deterrents, cybersecurity.
  - `Pole 4 ($162^\circ$, Upper Left)`: **Ecology, Climate & Biodiversity** — Rewilding, carbon capture, ocean restoration, regenerative agricultural transition.
- **Optimal Widget**: **Regular Pentagram Multi-Affinity Widget** with simplex sum constraint ($\sum_{i=0}^4 w_i = 100\%$) and total budget intensity slider.

#### 3.3.2 Theories of Property & Ownership Pentad
- **Poles**:
  - `Apex 0`: **Private Absolute (Allodial / Capitalist)** — Alienable individual title with unlimited accumulation rights.
  - `Pole 1`: **State Owned (Nationalized / Public)** — Managed by government ministries for the public interest.
  - `Pole 2`: **Common Pool Resource (Ostromian Commons)** — Self-governed by community user groups under clear nested rules.
  - `Pole 3`: **Usufruct (Possession by Use)** — Property rights exist only so long as land or capital is actively utilized.
  - `Pole 4`: **Open-Access / Copyleft (Digital Commons)** — Non-rivalrous commons where information and technology cannot be locked by patents.
- **Optimal Widget**: Regular Pentagram.

### 3.4 6-Pole Hexads ($\Delta^5$) — Diametric Regular Hexagon Circumplex Widget

#### 3.4.1 Macro Political Matrix Hexad (`topic-political-matrix-hexad`)
Six distinct civilizational operating systems paired along 3 diametric tension axes ($180^\circ$ oppositions):
- **Axis 1 (Capital vs. Labor)**: Free-Market Capitalism ($90^\circ$) vs. Democratic Socialism ($270^\circ$).
- **Axis 2 (Expertise vs. Locality)**: Technocratic Meritocracy ($30^\circ$) vs. Communitarian Localism ($210^\circ$).
- **Axis 3 (Control vs. Freedom)**: Authoritarian Order ($330^\circ$) vs. Libertarian Voluntaryism ($150^\circ$).
- **Poles**:
  - `Vertex 0 ($90^\circ$, Top)`: **Free-Market Capitalism** — Price system, private enterprise, shareholder returns, market discipline.
  - `Vertex 1 ($30^\circ$, Upper Right)`: **Technocratic Meritocracy** — Algorithmic optimization, cognitive elites, institutional competence over rhetoric.
  - `Vertex 2 ($330^\circ$, Lower Right)`: **Authoritarian Order** — Strong state, cultural cohesion, social hierarchy, strict enforcement of law.
  - `Vertex 3 ($270^\circ$, Bottom)`: **Democratic Socialism** — Social ownership of key commanding heights, economic democracy, workplace parity.
  - `Vertex 4 ($210^\circ$, Lower Left)`: **Communitarian Localism** — Small-scale bioregionalism, agrarian self-sufficiency, decentralized direct democracy.
  - `Vertex 5 ($150^\circ$, Upper Left)`: **Libertarian Voluntaryism** — Radical individual autonomy, non-aggression principle, contract-based society.
- **Optimal Widget**: Hexagonal Circumplex Slider with Diametric Tension Lines.

#### 3.4.2 Foreign Policy & Geopolitical Grand Strategy Hexad
- **Poles**:
  - `Vertex 0 ($90^\circ$)`: **Realpolitik / Hegemony** — National interest defined by relative military and economic dominance.
  - `Vertex 1 ($30^\circ$)`: **Liberal Internationalism** — Rule-based international order, multilateral treaties, UN, WTO, NATO.
  - `Vertex 2 ($330^\circ$)`: **Civilizational Realignment** — Multipolar alliance with culturally civilizational spheres (Huntington model).
  - `Vertex 3 ($270^\circ$)`: **Anti-Imperialist Solidarity** — Global South non-alignment, anti-colonial resistance to Western hegemony.
  - `Vertex 4 ($210^\circ$)`: **Strategic Neo-Isolationism** — Military retrenchment, non-interventionism, focus on domestic fortress integrity.
  - `Vertex 5 ($150^\circ$)`: **Cosmopolitan Borderless Globalism** — Abolition of national borders, universal human rights citizenship.
- **Optimal Widget**: Hexagonal Circumplex.

### 3.5 7-Pole Heptads ($\Delta^6$) — Heptagram & Radial Wheel Widget

#### 3.5.1 Seven Theories of Political Legitimacy
Where does the moral authority to govern originate?
- **Poles**:
  - `Pole 0 ($90^\circ$)`: **Divine Right / Cosmic Order** — Government authorized by sacred decree, religious doctrine, or cosmic mandate.
  - `Pole 1 ($38.57^\circ$)`: **Social Contract (Hobbes / Locke / Rousseau)** — Citizens rationally delegate coercive authority to protect life and property.
  - `Pole 2 ($347.14^\circ$)`: **Democratic Majoritarianism** — Legitimacy derived solely from periodic, transparent elections reflecting majority will.
  - `Pole 3 ($295.71^\circ$)`: **Institutional Functionalism (Output Legitimacy)** — Government is legitimate if and only if it delivers peace, prosperity, and clean water.
  - `Pole 4 ($244.29^\circ$)`: **Technocratic Epistocracy** — Authority belongs to those with demonstrated scientific, economic, and moral competence.
  - `Pole 5 ($192.86^\circ$)`: **Revolutionary Vanguard** — Authority justified by leading historical transformation toward human liberation.
  - `Pole 6 ($141.43^\circ$)`: **Anarchist Voluntaryism** — No government is legitimate; only unanimous, uncoerced individual consent creates binding obligation.
- **Optimal Widget**: **Heptagonal Legitimacy Wheel** with 7 radial sliders and central barycenter.
