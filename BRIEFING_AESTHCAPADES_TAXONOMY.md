## 1. Abstract & Scope

**Aesthcapades** maps human aesthetic commitments, taste preferences, and judgments of beauty. In traditional surveys, taste is collapsed into crude single-axis binaries (e.g. High vs. Low culture; Classical vs. Modern) or reduced to a checklist of favorite genres. 

Under the **Taxopolemics** engine, aesthetic positions are mapped to their natural geometric degrees of freedom: from **1-Pole scalars** (absolute affinity), to **2-Pole dialectical dipoles** (Representation vs. Abstraction; Hustle vs. Rest), to higher-order **Triads (3-Pole $\Delta^2$)**, **Tetrads (4-Pole $\Delta^3$)**, **Pentads (5-Pole $\Delta^4$)**, **Hexads (6-Pole $\Delta^5$)**, **Heptads (7-Pole $\Delta^6$)**, and **Octads (8-Pole $\Delta^7$)**.

**Engine cap:** the engine goes to **8 poles and caps there** — monopole through octad. The cap is a human limit, not a technical one: people cannot hold more than about eight positions in mind at once (Jason, 2026-11-14). This supersedes the 1–7 range in the earlier taxonomy briefings and the 1–6 range in the master spec.

**Scope note:** Aesthcapades is the fifth skin. It covers aesthetics and taste — where people have real positions, those positions are identity-forming, and comparing profiles is about compatibility (do we enjoy the same things, do we see beauty the same way). Food belongs here as a category, not as its own skin: food by itself would not carry a useful taxonomy for *discussion*, but it works for *exploration with friends* — and food is taste, literally (Jason, 2026-11-14).

## 2. Taxonomic Architecture & Rating Widget Assignments

### Category 1: Visual Art

#### 1.1 Representation vs Abstraction (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Representational ↔ Abstract
- **Question:** How much do you want art to look like the world vs. depart from it?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 1.2 Classical vs Contemporary (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Classical / Traditional ↔ Contemporary / Avant-garde
- **Question:** Do you trust the old masters or the new edge?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 1.3 Photography: Document vs Art (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **Documentary** — Truth, record, witness.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Fine Art** — Expression, composition, vision.
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Vernacular** — Snapshot, memory, the everyday.
- **Question:** What is a photograph for?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget.

#### 2.2 Live vs Recorded (2-Pole Dipole)

#### 2.3 Pop vs Art vs Classical (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)

### Category 6: Taste & Judgment

#### 6.1 High vs Low Culture (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **High / Canon** — The consecrated, the curriculum.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Popular / Mainstream** — The widely loved, the charts.
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Low / Kitsch** — The gutter, the guilty pleasure.
- **Question:** Where do you place your taste — the canon, the mainstream, or the gutter?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget.

#### 6.2 Canon vs Personal Taste (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Trust the canon ↔ Trust your own taste
- **Question:** When the canon and your gut disagree, who wins?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 6.3 Minimalism vs Maximalism (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Less is more ↔ More is more
- **Question:** The meta-aesthetic stance — the one that predicts most of the others.
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 6.4 Kitsch & Camp (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **Sincere enjoyment** — You love it straight.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Ironic appreciation** — You love it because it's bad.
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Rejection** — You don't love it at all.
- **Question:** Do you love kitsch, love it ironically, or not at all?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget.

#### 6.5 Theories of Beauty (4-Pole Tetrad $\Delta^3$)
- **Poles:**
  - `North (Green #22c55e)`: **Classical Harmony** — Symmetry, order, proportion.
  - `East (Yellow #eab308)`: **Sublime** — Awe, vastness, terror (Burke, Kant).
  - `South (Red #ef4444)`: **Picturesque** — Roughness, variety, irregularity.
  - `West (Blue #3b82f6)`: **Grotesque** — The ugly as aesthetic value.
- **Question:** What kind of beauty moves you?
- **Optimal Widget:** Orthogonal Diamond Deformation Polygon.

### Category 7: Food & Flavor

#### 7.1 Adventurous vs Cautious Eating (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Adventurous (try anything) ↔ Cautious (stick to the familiar)
- **Question:** Do you want the weird restaurant or the safe place? The classic friends-compatibility axis.
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 7.2 Mild vs Fiery (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Mild ↔ Fiery
- **Question:** How much heat do you want? Spice tolerance as a preference axis.
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.

#### 7.3 Comfort vs Casual vs Fine (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **Comfort** — Hearty, familiar, home.
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Casual** — Everyday, accessible, the neighborhood spot.
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Fine** — Refined, experimental, the tasting menu.
- **Question:** What register of food do you live in?
- **Optimal Widget:** Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget.

#### 7.4 Five Tastes (5-Pole Pentad $\Delta^4$)
- **Poles:**
  - `Pole 0 (Pink #ec4899)`: **Sweet**
  - `Pole 1 (Gray #e5e7eb)`: **Salty**
  - `Pole 2 (Yellow-green #a3e635)`: **Sour**
  - `Pole 3 (Dark green #166534)`: **Bitter**
  - `Pole 4 (Amber #92400e)`: **Umami**
- **Question:** The five basic tastes, as a five-pole star. How much do you seek each one?
- **Optimal Widget:** Pentad radial star. The first pentad in Aesthcapades, and a clean one — the five-taste taxonomy is well-established science.

#### 7.5 Dietary Identity (6-Pole Hexad $\Delta^5$)
- **Poles (in ring order, by degree of animal-product exclusion):**
  - `Pole 0 (Red #ef4444)`: **Carnivore** — Meat only.
  - `Pole 1 (Orange #f97316)`: **Omnivore** — Everything.
  - `Pole 2 (Blue #3b82f6)`: **Pescatarian** — Fish, no land meat.
  - `Pole 3 (Green #22c55e)`: **Vegetarian** — No meat, dairy and eggs ok.
  - `Pole 4 (Dark green #166534)`: **Vegan** — No animal products.
  - `Pole 5 (Purple #a855f7)`: **Fruitarian** — Fruit only.
- **Question:** What do you eat — and what won't you?
- **Optimal Widget:** Hexagonal ring. Adjacent poles are the most similar; opposites are the most different. A blend captures flexitarianism naturally (70% Omnivore, 30% Vegetarian).
- **Note:** Ovo-vegetarian folds into Vegetarian as a variant (eggs yes, dairy no) — it would crowd the Vegetarian pole as a separate point. A heptad (adding Ovo-vegetarian as a 7th pole) is possible if the Principal Investigator wants it explicit.

#### 7.6 Keto (1-Pole Monopole)
- **Pole:** Keto adherence, 0–100.
- **Question:** How strictly do you follow keto?
- **Optimal Widget:** Monopole slider.
- **Note:** Keto is a macronutrient approach (high fat, low carb), not an exclusion category — it cross-cuts the hexad. You can be keto-omnivore, keto-vegetarian, keto-vegan. So it sits on its own axis, orthogonal to 7.5, rather than as a pole on the same simplex.

#### 7.7 Cook vs Eat Out (2-Pole Dipole)
- **Poles:** Cook (make it yourself) ↔ Eat Out (be cooked for / go out)
- **Question:** Do you prefer to cook, or to be cooked for?
- **Optimal Widget:** Dipole slider.
