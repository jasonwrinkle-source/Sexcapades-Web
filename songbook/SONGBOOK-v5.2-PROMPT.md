*The master prompt for the next version of the songbook. Hand this whole
document to the songwriter (YoBiche, or any successor hand) and it
produces the v5.2 songbook: a universal song set that belongs to every

## What v5.2 is

The songbook grows up from albums-about-parts (the paper, the explainer,
the engine) to the shape of the thing itself: **one engine, many
maps**. Two tiers:

1. **The Universal Set** — songs that are true of TaxoPolemics and
   flex.capades.io as a whole, sung the same way on every skin. This was
   the focus of v5.1. When the app needs a song and a skin has none of
   its own, it falls back skin → Flexcapades → TaxoPolemics; the
   Universal Set is the Flexcapades tier, written to be at home
   everywhere.
2. **Per-Skin Songs** — one signature song per skin, in that skin's own
   voice, about that skin's own question. One song per map.

## Standing rules (do not break)

- **Lyrics are independent of style** (Jason's rule, 2026-10-22). Every
  track file holds lyrics only and names its treatment; each set's
  `STYLE.md` holds the treatment strings to paste into Suno's style
  box. Never bake a style into a lyric sheet.
- **All songs SFW by construction.** Even the Sexcapades signature song:
  warm, frank, funny — never explicit. These songs are the public face.
- **Terms that are law:** the map is a **hypermap**; the engine is
  **TaxoPolemics**; the application is **Flexcapades** (site:
  flex.capades.io; brand dotted form Flex.Capades.IO in display copy).
  The three operations are **Cross**, **Straight**, and **Circle
  Minus** — there is no umbrella term for them other than "operations".
  A pair is written ⋈ / ⊙ in print; in lyrics, sing the words. Never
  "cast". Taxonomy items are referred to by name, never by ID.
- **Plain words over jargon.** If a term needs a footnote, the lyric is
  wrong. The one exception: a term may appear if the line itself teaches
  it (the flagship does this with "hypermap").
- **Length:** each song a ~3-minute cut (320–450 words of pure lyric),
  Suno section tags ([Verse], [Chorus], [Bridge], [Outro]) in the sheet.
- **Meter words** (each skin's own word for what it measures) may season
  that skin's song: interest, stance, belief, taste, understanding,
  practice, experience, position, inclination, conviction, groove, fun,
  perspective, sensibility, discipline, stake.
- **Never state the number of skins or hypermaps** (Jason's rule,
  2026-11-24). They only grow, so any count in a lyric is stale on
  arrival. Sing "many doors", "one house, many doors" — never a total.
  Counts belong in `COVERAGE.md` only, as a dated snapshot with a
  stated method, and never in a lyric.

## The Universal Set (write these first)

One shared treatment for the whole set — extend the explainer's E1
treatment in the set's `STYLE.md` (bright, plainspoken, built to be
understood on first hearing) and give the flagship a second, anthemic
treatment as an alternate.

### U0 — "Ten Questions In" (the introduction; the entry door)

**Subject: the entry door itself — what a new person does first.**
Not a tour of the rooms and not a list of features: one person's
story, one arc, plain words, no terms. Open on the biggest questions
across maps — Fores (God), Polis (what you stand for, who holds the
power), Philo (how you know), and desire (what you want) — the ones
the broadest audience already has a stake in. Be honest about the door: a handful of
answers, about ten ratings, before the map starts to speak — one
rating is a dot, ten is a shape. Then carry the uses as beats in that
one story, in order: the map speaking (a lean you can see); the shape
you resemble — the "what am I" moment, the map pointing to an
archetype; wandering the archetypes to find your people; the record
written down (Scribe); the meeting laid out, hour and place (Scene
Setter); the fault lines watched so the talk lands where it matters
(Scene Sentinel); and a year later, answering again, the old map
still underneath the new — the drift (palimpsest). The chorus is the
door itself: *ten questions in, the map starts to speak.* Do not
name the tools as products and do not use the terms (archetype,
palimpsest, hypermap) — render them in plain words ("shape," "the
old map's still there, underneath the new"). The song must feel like
a story, not a catalogue wearing a chorus.

### U1 — "What It's For" (the flagship; working title)

**Subject: the use cases of TaxoPolemics and flex.capades.io.** Not
what the machinery is — what a person *does* with it. One use case per
verse, each verse a small scene with people in it, the chorus opening
with the question at the door — *Compare hypermaps?* — and returning
to the single claim: *you can see where you stand, and where somebody
else stands too, and the seeing is priced true.*

Verses (cover at least six of these):

1. **Finding your people** — rating your desires on the first map and
   finding the person whose map fits yours, before the awkward
   conversation, with limits honored by construction.
2. **Knowing yourself first** — the map as a mirror: most people start
   by rating, and learn what they actually hold (and how much it
   matters to them) by being asked pole by pole. ("Sitting at the table
   late at night alone / Rating every question like a mirror of your
   own.")
3. **The difficult conversation** — two people who disagree about
   politics or faith opening their maps instead of their throats:
   shared ground, real differences, and the receipts to show which is
   which.
4. **Negotiation** — two maps on the table before a scene, a project, a
   marriage: what is shared, what is complementary, what is off the
   table — all of it visible, none of it guessed. ("Before we sign the
   project or the vows we mean.")
5. **Work that fits** — a person's vocation map against a job's map:
   the gap, and the dealbreakers, seen before the interview.
6. **The chapter** — a local group passing sync cards hand to hand,
   phones in pockets, maps on paper; the fractal seed: every card a
   door back to the site.
7. **Watching yourself change** — the palimpsest: open the map you rated
   a year ago, layers stacked, and rate it again; the record of who
   you've been.
8. **The whole table** — a group of maps, a roster of friends: the engine
   reads the many the way it reads two, and the meeting gets priced
   for all of you.

Bridge: the engine under all of it — one engine, many maps; TaxoPolemics
named once, plainly, as "the engine that prices a meeting of minds."
Outro: the invitation — *bring your map*, and the question at the door
again.

### U2 — "One Engine, Many Maps"

The family song: many doors, one house. Each skin named in the verses
(sex.capades.io last, the rest in a singable order), one line of
character per map, no total anywhere. Flexcapades in verse one ("at the
center where the map teaches itself"), Sumer in verse two. Chorus: the
engine doesn't care what the conversation is about; it cares that the
conversation can be had honestly. The count-law line stays: "We never
count the skins in stone — tomorrow there'll be more."

### U3 — "Rate What You Hold"

The rating act itself, sung as instructions a friend could follow:
pick a pole, say how far you lean, then say how much it matters — and
how wide you listen. The speaking and the listening are two different
numbers, both yours alone, and neither is the same question as the
lean. Brightness as the picture: what matters most shines; what you
can't stand shows in its opposite colors. A late verse lets the singer
load an archetype or borrow a sample — its emblem in hand. Keep the
seed line: "You don't have to rate every seed that is sown."

### U4 — "Three Operations"

Cross, Straight, Circle Minus in plain words, one verse each:
Cross — what we complete in each other, the communion; the verse
names the krossmatch grid and the depth factor that prices the
meeting **in a single line** (the kink factor on the sex map, the same
design);
Straight — the ground we share, the interesting conversation, sung
as the converse;
Circle Minus — where we split, and what the split is worth, sung as
an indaba: a meeting to talk it through. The bridge keeps the
measured-gap couplet: the gap is measured, the numbers show the size
of the space.
Chorus: three ways two maps can meet — and a tableful meets the same
way — and every meeting gets its own number. Outro: "Where we meet and
deliberate."

### U5 — "Bring Your Map"

The social song: sync cards, chapters, munches, the friend who hands
you a card instead of a pitch — or loads a roster pack and brings the
whole room in. Fractal seeding sung as a round-like
refrain — every singer hands the song to the next singer.

### U6 — "The Meeting After the Meeting" (carried over)

The existing explainer track of this name joins the Universal Set
unchanged; it is the set's closer. Note the carry-over on its sheet
rather than rewriting it. (Still a stub in v5.2 — writing it is a
separate job.)

### U7 — "Many Views Under One Roof" (v5.1.2, from AntiGravity's Track II; v5.2 reframed as a scene)

How a pair or group actually uses the views across an evening, one
verse per surface: the gate and the picker (the question mark opens
the Docs pull-down; About is a double-click on the skin name; on the
meta map the picker is already in view), the hypermap and its placard
(a cross slider for the pair, a polygon for the rest; heat from
Withdrawn to Burning, or Turned off to Horndog on the adult map; the
cooler member sets the flame), then Hot List (search and filter down
the shortlist), Scene Setter (opening, flow, and close; boundaries and
aftercare), Scribe (the dialogue where the positions meet, or a story
or a play guide — stance maps get the dialogue, activity maps the
story), Scene Sentinel (check-ins, timers, and the watch), and Scan or
Import. Chorus: many views under one roof, and your work never drops.
Bridge: the manual, the Glossary ("The Glossary keeps every term on
solid ground"), and the Quick Start (about ten minutes, three per
branch to rate).

### U8 — "Cards, Sheets, Packs & Veils" (v5.1.2, from AntiGravity's Track IV; v5.2 adds the scan scene)

How hypermaps travel without a central cloud: the pocket sync card
(three-point-five by two), the black-on-white QR sheet (to pin upon
the wall), the roster pack, Scan or Import (camera, image, or JSON),
and the veil — Revealed to Concealed, filtering what the spokes
display without erasing a thing, a couple's group profile wearing its
own outward ring, staying a unit on the floor with the cooler member's
heat in store. The scan verse: "She points her camera at your card
across the coffee cup / No login wall, no password form, the spokes
just blossom up." Chorus closes on the question at the door: *Compare
hypermaps?*

## Per-Skin Songs (one each)

One signature song per skin: the skin's question, sung in its own
register, its meter word in the chorus. ~3 minutes each, same sheet
format. The skins:

- **Sexcapades** (interest) — desire with its limits honored; the first
  map; playful, frank, SFW. The bridge names the kink categories and
  kink lines ("Kink categories fan out into the kink lines"); the hanky
  rides on the placard in verse two.
- **Poliscapades** (conviction) — a citizenry that could show its work:
  where the parties actually stand, pole by pole.
- **Forescapades** (belief) — faith: one God, many houses, and the
  oldest layer, Sumer, beneath them; the conversation the faiths keep
  almost having. A late verse sings Sumer: the clay tablets, the
  eight-pointed star, the fresh waters below.
- **Aesthcapades** (taste) — what you find beautiful and why; taste as a
  position you can defend.
- **Chemcapades** (understanding) — how molecules combine; bonding as
  the oldest cross-match there is. The bridge runs from the flask on
  the bench to the periodic chart.
- **Dharmacapades** (practice) — the path walked, not argued; practice
  over position.
- **Expericapades** (experience) — how experiments are done; the null
  result gets its verse.
- **Mattercapades** (understanding) — the stuff things are made of,
  from baseload to metamaterials.
- **Philoscapades** (position) — the old questions, mapped: mind and
  body, free will, what a good life is.
- **Praxicapades** (practice) — how work gets done; modeling the world
  to change it.
- **Quantacapades** (understanding) — what the wavefunction is; a
  century of interpretation as a family argument.
- **Simulcapades** (stance) — copies and what's real; the original and
  the simulation walk into a bar.
- **Techcapades** (stance) — the tools we live inside; who holds the
  keys to the platforms.
- **Theoricapades** (position) — theory itself: what it's for, when a
  beautiful one is wrong. "The theory's a compass we hold in the sea"
  — never "a net that we cast" (both words are retired).
- **Vocapades** (inclination) — the work a life wants; fit, risk, and
  the price of a calling.
- **Nouscapades** (conviction) — what counts as a mind; the dish, the
  frog, the robot, and the question that won't sit down.
- **Dancecapades** (groove) — lead, follow, and move together; the ask,
  the tanda, and the conversation two people have without saying a
  word. The chorus names the Cross ("Where your lead meets my follow,
  that's the Cross we are on"); the bridge corrects the lead-centric
  framing ("A dip is a trust fall, you ask before you lean / And the
  follow's not silent, she's answering between").
- **Questcapades** (fun) — the tales a table tells; session zero, the
  forever GM, and the final boss called scheduling. Name the real
  archetypes only — One-Shot Wanderer, Method Actor, Lore Keeper —
  never invented ones; the Friday Knights roster pack is the party
  that loads. The bridge: "And it lives in the space between the dice
  and the hand."
- **Biocapades** (perspective) — what counts as alive, and what the
  living world asks of us; the gene, the whole, and the hand that
  edits life. The chorus runs from the virus at the door to the whale
  in the deep; the operations couplet prices reckless remaking against
  stewardship.
- **Linguacapades** (sensibility) — who sets the rules of the tongue,
  and what your words say about you; the dialect, the drift, and the
  words that won't translate. "Emoji is a script and the shibboleth a
  thorn." The operations couplet prices the pedants against the story.
- **Fastcapades** (discipline) — the discipline of not eating; the empty
  plate, the window, and the hunger that teaches. "Is it prayer at the
  window or the clinic's own chart?" The chorus names the clarity of
  hunger; the bridge names the archetypes by their full names
  (Ramadan Observer).
- **Flexcapades** (stake) — the map that is its own manual; the usage
  wing and the advisory wing; the meta skin, home of
  flex.capades.io. The bridge names the six roles: Lawgiver, Witness,
  Builder, Tester, Munch Regular, Lurker — "Six roles around the
  circle, each one with a stake to show." The brief keeps the
  provenance note (built from AntiGravity's Track III flex verses).

## Deliverables & format

For the Universal Set: a folder `universal-set/` with `STYLE.md` (the
shared treatment + the flagship's alternate), one `.md` per track
(U0–U8) holding lyrics only, each headed by title, **house byline**
(Jason Mark Wrinkle & Yolanda Biche et al.), treatment name, and a
one-line brief. For the skins: `per-skin/` with one `.md` per skin,
same shape (byline on every sheet); a shared per-skin `STYLE.md` may
hold one treatment per skin or a single adaptable treatment with
per-skin notes. A `SUNO-PASTE-KIT.md` per set: which box gets what, in
order. A `COVERAGE.md` proves, by grep, that every glossary term is
sung somewhere — with hit counts, line locations, a skin/topic count
table parsed from the JSON (a dated snapshot, never a lyric), and a
word-count audit for the ~3-minute cut, all with a stated method so
the audit can be re-run. Update the songbook index to carry v5.2 at
the top: version, date, the two tiers, and the fallback chain (a skin's
own song first, then the Universal Set, then the TaxoPolemics album).
