# Expericapades
**Byline:** Jason Mark Wrinkle & Yolanda Biche et al.
**Treatment:** Indie pop-rock, bright and curious, driven acoustic guitars, snappy percussion
**Brief:** How experiments are done; the null result gets its verse.
