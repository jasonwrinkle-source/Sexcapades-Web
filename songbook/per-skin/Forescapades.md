# Forescapades
**Byline:** Jason Mark Wrinkle & Yolanda Biche et al.
**Treatment:** Solemn acoustic folk, reverent, piano and cello, steady build
**Brief:** Faith: one God, many houses — and the oldest layer, Sumer, beneath them; the conversation the faiths keep almost having.

[Verse]
Three houses built upon the same deep stone
Three ways of knowing that we aren't alone
We look at the sky and we name the same star
But we draw different lines for how close and how far
The brothers have argued for two thousand years
Through the texts and the triumphs, the schisms and tears
But open the hypermap, let the colors align
And see where your belief sits next to mine
