# Theoricapades
**Byline:** Jason Mark Wrinkle & Yolanda Biche et al.
**Treatment:** Intricate chamber pop, pizzicato strings, playful piano, precise vocal harmonies
**Brief:** Theory itself: what it's for, when a beautiful one is wrong.
