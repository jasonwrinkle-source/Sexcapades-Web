# Linguacapades
**Treatment:** Playful chamber-pop with wordplay and layered harmonies, a song that loves the sound of its own sentences
**Brief:** Who sets the rules of the tongue, and what your words say about you; the dialect, the drift, and the words that won't translate.

Every generation gets to choose
