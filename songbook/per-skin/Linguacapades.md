# Linguacapades
**Byline:** Jason Mark Wrinkle & Yolanda Biche et al.
**Treatment:** Playful chamber-pop with wordplay and layered harmonies, a song that loves the sound of its own sentences
**Brief:** Who sets the rules of the tongue, and what your words say about you; the dialect, the drift, and the words that won't translate.

[Verse]

The schoolbook sets a rule, and the streetcar finds its grooves

Code-switch at the doorway when you cross into the town
Slang is the weather and the slang becomes the news
Every generation gets a vocabulary to choose
Open up the hypermap and listen to the sound
Of the living ways of speaking where the meanings can be found
