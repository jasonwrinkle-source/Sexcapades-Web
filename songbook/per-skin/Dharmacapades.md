# Dharmacapades
**Byline:** Jason Mark Wrinkle & Yolanda Biche et al.
**Treatment:** Acoustic world music, hypnotic percussion, resonant vocals, sitar and guitar elements
**Brief:** The path walked, not argued; practice over position.

A path that you walk when you're out there alone
So open the hypermap, let the colors unfold
