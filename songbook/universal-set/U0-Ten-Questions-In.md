# U0 — Ten Questions In
**Byline:** Jason Mark Wrinkle & Yolanda Biche et al.
**Treatment:** E1 Treatment (Extended)
**Brief:** The introduction to the Universal Set — the entry door in song form. Opens on the biggest questions across maps (Fores — God; Polis — what you stand for, who holds the power; Philo — how you know; desire — what you want), honest about the door (a handful, about ten ratings before the map speaks), and carries the uses as beats in one person's story: the map speaking, the shape you resemble, wandering the archetypes, the record written, the meeting laid out, the fault lines watched, and the drift a year later.
