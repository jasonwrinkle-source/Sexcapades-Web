# U7 — Many Views Under One Roof
**Treatment:** E1 Treatment (Extended)
**Brief:** A guided tour of the views — gate, picker, hypermap, Hot List, Scene Setter, Scribe, Scene Sentinel, Scan or Import. Adapted from AntiGravity's Track II ("The Views Under One Roof"), errors fixed: the question mark opens the Docs pull-down (About is a double-click on the skin name), Quick Start is about ten minutes, no counts.

[Verse]
Step through the gate at the front of the house
Tap the question mark and the Docs drop down
Double-click the skin name if you want the About
The picker opens wide and the skins spill out
Each hypermap holds a world to rate
And the hostname steers you straight from the starting gate
Keep the header above you wherever you roam
Tap the view-switch button and every view feels like home

[Chorus]
Many views under one roof, and your work never drops
Switch away and come right back, the drafting never stops
From the radial hypermap to the Hot List on the screen
To Scene Setter, Scribe, and Scene Sentinel in between
One header at the top, one engine underneath
Every view in Flexcapades lets the conversation breathe

[Verse]
First view is the hypermap, a radial star of spokes
Fan a branch out to the leaves for the serious folks
Tap a leaf to pop the placard—that's where ratings live
A cross slider for the pair, a polygon for the rest
Set the flame of your heat from Withdrawn up to Burning bright
Or Turned off up to Horndog on an adult night
And when a pair or group profile takes the stage as one
The cooler member sets the flame so nobody's overrun

[Verse]
Switch the view to Hot List when you want the ranked outline
Search and filter down the shortlist where the top matches shine

To moderate the ground rules and decide where we begin

Boundaries and aftercare that everybody knows
Scribe will write the dialogue where the positions meet
Or a story or a play guide, warm and sweet
Scene Sentinel keeps watch while the room goes live
Check-ins, timers, and the watch that helps the night thrive
And Scan or Import sits ready as an action on the bar
To read a sync card, QR sheet, or roster pack from afar
