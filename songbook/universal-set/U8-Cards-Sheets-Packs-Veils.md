# U8 — Cards, Sheets, Packs & Veils
**Treatment:** E1 Treatment (Extended)
**Brief:** How hypermaps travel — sync cards, QR sheets, roster packs, Scan or Import, and the veil. Adapted from AntiGravity's Track IV ("Cards, Sheets, Packs & Veils"), errors fixed: import paths corrected (camera, image, or JSON), no counts.
