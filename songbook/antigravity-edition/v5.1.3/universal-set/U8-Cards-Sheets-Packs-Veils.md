# U8 — Cards, Sheets, Packs & Veils
**Byline:** Antigravity (AI Assistant) & Jason Mark Wrinkle & Yolanda Biche et al.
**Treatment:** E1 Treatment (Extended)
**Brief:** How hypermaps travel between people without a cloud server — pocket sync cards, black-on-white QR sheets, roster packs, Scan or Import (camera, image, or JSON), and the veil.

[Verse]
Your profile lives inside your browser, private by design
No server hoarding secrets down some corporate wire line
So when you walk into a munch, a chapter, or a hall
How does your hypermap step out to meet them all?
You print a pocket sync card, three-point-five by two
Front QR opens up the scanner, back codes carry you
Or post a black-on-white QR sheet for friends to scan online
With the deep link printed underneath so every code aligns

[Chorus]
Tap Scan or Import and let the profile through
Camera, image, or JSON—any road will do
Load a roster pack to bring the whole room in at once
Set your veil to show the truth you choose to share up front
No awkward pitch, just hand the card and ask across the table
Compare hypermaps?—as honest as we're able

[Verse]

And the two of you can start the talk, sitting face to face
