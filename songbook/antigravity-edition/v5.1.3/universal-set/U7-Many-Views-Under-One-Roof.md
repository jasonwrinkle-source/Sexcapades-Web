# U7 — Many Views Under One Roof
**Byline:** Antigravity (AI Assistant) & Jason Mark Wrinkle & Yolanda Biche et al.
**Treatment:** E1 Treatment (Extended)
**Brief:** How a pair or group actually uses the views across an evening — from the gate, picker, and hypermap placard to Hot List, Scene Setter, Scribe, Scene Sentinel, and Scan or Import.

[Verse]
You step through the gate when you arrive for the night
Or hostname routing steers you straight to the site
On the meta map the picker is already in view
With a door for each question that is waiting for you
Tap the question mark up top and the Docs drop down
Double-click the skin name when you want the About in town
Keep that header above you wherever you roam
Tap the view-switch button and every view feels like home

[Chorus]
Many views under one roof, and your work never drops
Switch away and come right back, the drafting never stops
From the radial hypermap to the Hot List on the screen
To Scene Setter, Scribe, and Scene Sentinel in between
One header at the top, one engine underneath
Every view in Flexcapades lets the conversation breathe

[Verse]
We start on the hypermap, a radial star of spokes
And fan a branch out to the leaves for the curious folks
We tap a leaf to pop the placard—that's where ratings live
With a cross slider for a pair, or a polygon to give
You set the flame of your heat from Withdrawn up to Burning bright
Or Turned off up to Horndog on an adult night
And when our pair or group profile takes the stage as one
The cooler member sets the flame so nobody's overrun

[Verse]
We switch the view to Hot List just to see what tops the chart
Then open up Scene Setter so the planning gets a start
Two profiles at the table, setting ground rules in a row
The opening, the boundaries, the aftercare we know
We bounce back to the hypermap to check a single line
And our half-built agreement is still waiting, safe and fine
Then Scribe writes out the dialogue or story that we share
While Scene Sentinel keeps watch with timers and with care

[Bridge]

To read a sync card, QR sheet, or roster pack and spin

Or visit the engine page to see what makes it great
