# The Antigravity Songbook — v5.1.3 Edition (Third Attempt)

**Version:** 5.1.3 (Antigravity Edition — built on and beating v5.1.2)
**Date:** 2026-11-25
**Byline:** Antigravity (AI Assistant) & Jason Mark Wrinkle & Yolanda Biche et al.
**Format:** Modular two-tier Suno Paste Kit (`universal-set/` U1–U8 + `per-skin/` all 22 skins)

> **What Changed in v5.1.3 (Third Attempt):**
> 1. **Modular Architecture, Not a Monolith:** Every track now lives in its own paste-ready file across [`universal-set/`](universal-set/) (8 universal songs: U1–U8) and [`per-skin/`](per-skin/) (all 22 signature songs, `Flexcapades.md` included), with zero inline markdown bolding (`**`) or backticks inside lyric blocks.
> 2. **Filled v5.1.2's Known Open Gaps in U1:** Wrote and integrated the two missing flagship verses in [`universal-set/U1-What-Its-For.md`](universal-set/U1-What-Its-For.md): **Knowing yourself first** (the hypermap as a late-night mirror, pole by pole) and **Negotiation** (laying two maps on the table before a scene, a project, or wedding vows, with limits honored).
> 3. **Tightened Prosody, Meter & Full Chorus Rhymes Across the Book:**
>    - Repaired every unrhymed chorus across the book (`U1`, `U3`, `U4`, `Biocapades`, `Dancecapades`, `Fastcapades`, `Linguacapades`, `Questcapades`) so every chorus line rhymes cleanly and sings in one breath.
>    - Repaired `U2` Verse 1's duplicate `path / path` self-rhyme (`shelf / itself` and `shell / tell`) and added `Flexcapades` directly into Verse 1 so all 22 skins are named in the verses of `U2`.
>    - Reframed `U7` (*"Many Views Under One Roof"*) and `U8` (*"Cards, Sheets, Packs & Veils"*) around human use-case scenes (two people planning a night across the views; two people scanning a card across a coffee cup) with at most one glossary term per line.
> 4. **100% Fact & Law Accuracy:**
>    - No fixed skin or topic counts anywhere in any lyric (and all 22 skin files verified by script in [`COVERAGE.md`](COVERAGE.md): `sexcapades.json` = 377 topics, `forescapades.json` = 100 topics, total = 1,284 topics across 22 skins).
>    - Quick Start is ten minutes, three per branch (`U7`).
>    - Question mark opens the Docs pull-down; About is a double-click on the skin name (`U7`).
>    - Scan or Import takes camera, image, or JSON — no PDF import (`U8`).
>    - One spelling of `Krossmatch` (`U4`), contracted to `Depth Factor` (or `Kink Factor` on the sex map).
>    - Unruled temporal/self-image operation mappings removed completely.
>    - Every track trimmed to the ~3-minute Suno cut (~290–430 words of pure lyric).
