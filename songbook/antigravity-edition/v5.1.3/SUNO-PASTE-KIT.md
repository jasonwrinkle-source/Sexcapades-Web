# Suno Paste Kit — Songbook v5.1.3 (Antigravity Edition)

Every song in `universal-set/` and `per-skin/` is a paste-ready ~3-minute cut (`[Verse]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Bridge]`, `[Outro]` tags only, zero inline markdown bolding or code ticks in the lyric blocks).

## Step-by-Step Paste Order in Suno (Custom Mode)

1. **Box 1 — Style of Music:**
   - **For `U1-What-Its-For.md` (The Flagship):** Paste the **U1 Anthemic Alternate** from `universal-set/STYLE.md`:
     `Stadium indie rock, soaring vocals, building percussion, piano and guitars, hopeful, expansive, crowd singalong chorus`
   - **For `U2` through `U8` (`universal-set/`):** Paste the **E1 Treatment (Extended)** from `universal-set/STYLE.md`:
     `Bright acoustic pop, clear vocals, upbeat, plainspoken, driving rhythm, anthemic chorus, organic instruments, warm production`
   - **For any Per-Skin Signature Song (`per-skin/<Skin>.md`):** Paste that skin's style prompt from `per-skin/STYLE.md` (also printed on the `**Treatment:**` header line of each sheet).

2. **Box 2 — Lyrics:**
   - Copy everything from the first `[Verse]` tag down through the closing `[Outro]` block of the track's `.md` file.
   - Do **not** paste the `# Title`, `**Byline:**`, `**Treatment:**`, or `**Brief:**` header lines into the Lyrics box.

3. **Box 3 — Title:**
   - Paste the track title from the `#` heading at the top of the sheet.
