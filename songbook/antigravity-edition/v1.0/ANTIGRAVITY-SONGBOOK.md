# The Antigravity Songbook — TaxoPolemics, the `*.capades.io` Family & the Views of Flexcapades

**Version:** 1.0 (Antigravity Edition — distinct from YoBiche's v5.1 / v5.1.1 Songbook)
**Date:** 2026-11-25
**Author / Byline:** Antigravity (AI Assistant) & Jason Mark Wrinkle
**Format:** Suno Paste Kit ready (Style Treatments in Section 1; pure lyric sheets with `[Verse]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Bridge]`, `[Outro]` tags in Sections 2–4)

> **Design Note:** This songbook is written by **Antigravity** to complement—and stand clearly distinguished from—YoBiche's songbook. It weaves the official glossary terms throughout (**Hypermap**, **TaxoPolemics**, **Indaba**, **Commune / Converse**, **Crossmatch / Krossmatch**, **Depth Factor / Kink Factor**, **Veil**, **Palimpsest** and **Worldline**, **Archetypes and Samples**, **Emblem**, **Group Profile**, **Roster Pack**, **Sync Card**, **QR Sheet**, **Scan or Import**, **Quick Start**, **Scribe**, **Scene Setter**, **The Picker**, **Skin Menu**, **Hostname Routing**, **The Engine Page**, and *"Compare hypermaps?"*), omitting the retired term *net*. In accordance with standing rules, no exact number of hypermap skins or topics in a hypermap is ever stated (using open bounds like `>20` or `>400` where scale is sung), because the family and its taxonomies only grow.
