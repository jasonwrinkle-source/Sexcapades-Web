## 1. Abstract & Epistemic Scope

**Philoscapades** maps human intellectual commitments, metaphysical worldviews, normative frameworks, and epistemological postures. In traditional surveys, philosophical inclinations are collapsed into crude single-axis binaries (e.g. Rationalist vs. Empiricist; Deontologist vs. Utilitarian). 

Under the **Taxopolemics** engine, philosophical concepts are mapped to their natural geometric degrees of freedom: from **1-Pole scalars** (absolute affinity), to **2-Pole dialectical dipoles** (Thesis vs. Antithesis / Giving arguments vs. Inquiring), to higher-order **Triads (3-Pole $\Delta^2$)**, **Tetrads (4-Pole $\Delta^3$)**, **Pentads (5-Pole $\Delta^4$)**, **Hexads (6-Pole $\Delta^5$)**, and **Heptads (7-Pole $\Delta^6$)**.

### Category 1: Epistemology & Theory of Truth

#### 1.1 The Agrippan Trilemma / Justification Triad (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles**:
  - `Pole 0 (Apex)`: **Foundationalism** — Basic self-evident beliefs justify all derived beliefs without infinite regress (Descartes, foundational axioms).
  - `Pole 1 (Base Right)`: **Coherentism** — Beliefs justify one another in a holistic, mutually reinforcing web of belief (Quine, Sellars).
  - `Pole 2 (Base Left)`: **Infinitism** — Justification is an open-ended, infinite non-repeating chain of rational warrants (Klein).
- **Optimal Widget**: **Barycentric Equilateral Triangle Widget** with center midline readout and uniform intensity slider.

#### 1.2 Epistemic Grounds & Justification Matrix (4-Pole Tetrad $\Delta^3$)
- **Poles**:
  - `North (Green #22c55e)`: **Pure Rationalism** (*A priori* deduction, innate ideas, intellectual intuition; Leibniz, Spinoza).
  - `East (Yellow #eab308)`: **Radical Empiricism** (*A posteriori* sensory verification, induction, tabula rasa; Locke, Hume).
  - `South (Red #ef4444)`: **Pragmatism / Instrumentalism** (Truth is actionable utility and problem-solving efficacy; James, Dewey).
  - `West (Blue #3b82f6)`: **Skepticism / Fallibilism** (Certainty is impossible; all knowledge is provisional; Pyrrho, Popper).
- **Optimal Widget**: **Orthogonal Diamond Deformation Polygon (Concept B-alt2)**.

#### 1.3 Theories of Truth (4-Pole Tetrad $\Delta^3$)
- **Poles**:
  - `North`: **Correspondence Theory** (Truth is correspondence with objective mind-independent facts; Russell).
  - `East`: **Coherence Theory** (Truth is internal consistency within an epistemic system; Bradley).
  - `South`: **Pragmatic Theory** (Truth is that which proves fertile in historical praxis; Peirce).
  - `West`: **Deflationism / Redundancy** ("Truth" is a linguistic device, not a robust metaphysical property; Ramsey).
- **Optimal Widget**: **Orthogonal Diamond Polygon**.

### Category 2: Metaphysics & Ontology

#### 2.1 The Mind-Body Ontology Matrix (4-Pole Tetrad $\Delta^3$)
- **Poles**:
  - `North (Green)`: **Substance Dualism** (Mind and physical matter are distinct, mutually irreducible ontologies; Descartes).
  - `East (Yellow)`: **Physicalism / Materialism** (All reality is physical; mental phenomena reduce to brain states).
  - `South (Red)`: **Objective Idealism** (Consciousness is primary; the physical world is a cognitive manifestation; Berkeley, Hegel).
  - `West (Blue)`: **Neutral Monism / Dual-Aspect** (Mind and matter are two complementary aspects of one neutral substance; Spinoza, Russell).
- **Optimal Widget**: **Orthogonal Diamond Polygon**.

#### 2.2 Free Will, Agency & Determinism (4-Pole Tetrad $\Delta^3$)
- **Poles**:
  - `North`: **Hard Determinism** (Causal closure of the universe renders free will an illusion; Spinoza, Laplace).
  - `East`: **Libertarian Agency** (Conscious agents possess genuine causal origination; Kant, Chisholm).
  - `South`: **Classical Compatibilism** (Freedom is acting according to internal motives, even if determined; Hume).
  - `West`: **Hard Incompatibilism / Illusionism** (Free will is impossible under both determinism and indeterminism; Pereboom).
- **Optimal Widget**: **Orthogonal Diamond Polygon**.

#### 2.3 The Ontology of Universals (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles**:
  - `Pole 0`: **Platonic Realism** (Universals exist ante rem in an objective non-physical realm).
  - `Pole 1`: **Nominalism** (Only individual particulars exist; universals are mere names/conventions).
  - `Pole 2`: **Conceptualism** (Universals exist solely as conceptual frameworks within minds).
- **Optimal Widget**: **Barycentric Equilateral Triangle**.

#### 2.4 The Metaphysics of Time (3-Pole Triad $\Delta^2$)
- **Poles**:
  - `Pole 0`: **Presentism** (Only the present moment exists; past is gone, future is unformed).
  - `Pole 1`: **Eternalism (Block Universe)** (Past, present, and future exist simultaneously in 4D spacetime).
  - `Pole 2`: **Growing Block Universe** (Past and present exist; the future is actively becoming).
- **Optimal Widget**: **Barycentric Equilateral Triangle**.

### Category 3: Axiology & Normative Ethics

#### 3.1 The Grand Normative Ethical Quartet (4-Pole Tetrad $\Delta^3$)
- **Poles**:
  - `North (Green)`: **Deontology / Duty Ethics** (Categorical imperatives, rights, moral rules independent of outcome; Kant).
  - `East (Yellow)`: **Consequentialism / Utilitarianism** (Maximizing net flourishing, welfare, or utility; Bentham, Mill).
  - `South (Red)`: **Virtue Ethics** (Cultivating character, phronesis, and *eudaimonia*; Aristotle, MacIntyre).
  - `West (Blue)`: **Relational & Care Ethics** (Interdependence, contextual compassion, vulnerability; Gilligan, Noddings).
- **Optimal Widget**: **Orthogonal Diamond Polygon**.

#### 3.2 Meta-Ethics Compass (4-Pole Tetrad $\Delta^3$)
- **Poles**:
  - `North`: **Moral Realism / Objectivism** (Objective moral facts exist independently of human attitudes).
  - `East`: **Moral Relativism / Subjectivism** (Moral claims are true relative to cultural or individual standards).
  - `South`: **Moral Error Theory / Nihilism** (All moral propositions are cognitive but systematically false; Mackie).
  - `West`: **Expressivism / Non-Cognitivism** (Moral claims express non-truth-apt emotions or commands; Ayer, Blackburn).
- **Optimal Widget**: **Orthogonal Diamond Polygon**.

#### 3.3 Axiological Goods / What Has Intrinsic Value (4-Pole Tetrad $\Delta^3$)
- **Poles**:
  - `North`: **Hedonism** (Conscious pleasure and absence of pain are the sole intrinsic goods).
  - `East`: **Eudaimonism** (Human flourishing, virtue, and realization of rational potential).
  - `South`: **Desire-Satisfaction** (Fulfillment of individual subjective preferences and autonomous projects).
  - `West`: **Objective List Perfectionism** (Plurality of goods: knowledge, beauty, friendship, autonomy).
- **Optimal Widget**: **Orthogonal Diamond Polygon**.

#### 6.2 Seven Theories of Truth (7-Pole Heptad $\Delta^6$)
Where does ontological and propositional truth reside? Distinct from Poliscapades' *Seven Theories of Political Legitimacy*, Philoscapades grounds its epistemological heptad in the canonical theories of truth:
- **Poles**:
  - `Pole 0 (Red)`: **Correspondence Theory** (Truth is structural isomorphism between proposition and mind-independent reality; Aristotle, Russell).
  - `Pole 1 (Orange)`: **Coherence Theory** (Truth is systemic consistency within an interconnected web of propositions; Spinoza, Bradley, Blanshard).
  - `Pole 2 (Yellow)`: **Pragmatic Theory** (Truth is what works in experiential practice and fruitful inquiry; Peirce, James, Dewey).
  - `Pole 3 (Green)`: **Deflationary / Redundancy Theory** (Asserting truth adds no semantic content beyond asserting the sentence itself; Ramsey, Ayer).
  - `Pole 4 (Cyan)`: **Semantic Theory** (Truth defined across meta-language satisfaction criteria without paradox; Tarski).
  - `Pole 5 (Indigo)`: **Epistemic / Constructivist Theory** (Truth is ideal rational assertibility under conditions of unforced consensus; Dummett, Putnam, Habermas).
  - `Pole 6 (Violet)`: **Phenomenological / Disclosive Truth** (Aletheia — truth as unhiddenness, unconcealment, and primordial clearing; Heidegger).
- **Optimal Widget**: **7-Axis Heptagram / Regular Heptagon Polygon** (`"heptad-radar"`).
