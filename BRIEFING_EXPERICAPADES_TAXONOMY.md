## 1. Abstract & Scope

**Expericapades** maps human positions on experimental physics — the world of the detector, the ultra-high vacuum chamber, the cryogenic dilution refrigerator, and the particle collider. It is about how human beings probe physical nature to test hypotheses and discover new phenomena. In popular culture, experimental physics is often collapsed into either "testing theorists' homework" or building consumer electronics. In reality, experimental physics is an autonomous intellectual culture with its own epistemological standards, its own deep philosophical arguments, and its own physical realities: its core currency is the unambiguous physical signal separated from noise, and its central conflicts revolve around discovery standards (what is real?), scale (Big Science vs. Tabletop), methodology (theory-driven confirmation vs. agnostic exploration), and instrument building.

Under the **Taxopolemics** engine, experimental positions are mapped to their natural geometric degrees of freedom: from **1-Pole scalars** (absolute affinity), to **2-Pole dialectical dipoles** (Theory-Driven vs. Discovery-Driven; 5-Sigma vs. Bayesian), to higher-order **Triads (3-Pole $\Delta^2$)**, **Tetrads (4-Pole $\Delta^3$)**, **Pentads (5-Pole $\Delta^4$)**, **Hexads (6-Pole $\Delta^5$)**, **Heptads (7-Pole $\Delta^6$)**, and **Octads (8-Pole $\Delta^7$)**.

**Engine cap:** the engine goes to **8 poles and caps there** — monopole through octad. The cap is a human limit, not a technical one: people cannot hold more than about eight positions in mind at once (Jason, 2026-11-14).

**Scope note:** Expericapades is the experimental physics skin, one of four physics skins alongside **Quantacapades** (theoretical physics), **Simulcapades** (computational physics), and **Mattercapades** (applied physics). The four are different cultures with different values, and a single skin would dilute all of them. Expericapades is about interrogating nature to discover fundamental physical laws and phenomena — *"How does nature work at the empirical frontier?"* It is strictly distinct from **Mattercapades**, which asks *"How do we engineer materials and devices for practical performance?"*, and from **Simulcapades**, where experiments are virtual: real experiments wrestle with physical hardware, photons, stray capacitances, and cosmic ray backgrounds. The audience is narrower than the other skins — physicists and physics-adjacent people — but the positions are real and passionate, and for collaborators they determine whether you can work together.

**Name note:** The name is **Expericapades**, from *experiment* — the Principal Investigator's pick, from YoBiche's proposed set (Quantacapades / Expericapades / Simulcapades / Mattercapades, accepted 2026-11-15).

**Synthesis note:** This briefing merges YoBiche's Expericapades (broader, more attitudinal — the life, the attitudes, the culture of the lab) with Antigravity's Expericapades (17 topics — deeper, more technically specific, naming the methods, the hardware, and the canonical frontiers). Neither version alone is complete; together they are strong. Where both sources cover the same topic, the better version was kept, as noted per topic. Antigravity's pole-count table was wrong in her file (it claimed 18 topics and 15 dipoles; the actual count is 17 topics and 14 dipoles) — the counts in this briefing were verified by hand against the headings.
